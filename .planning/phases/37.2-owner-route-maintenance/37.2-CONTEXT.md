# Phase 37.2: Owner Route + Maintenance Mobile - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-03-30
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Bring route management and truck maintenance to the owner mobile portal. Route management: list routes, view route detail (stops/driver/truck/status), create and edit routes. Truck maintenance: view service history per truck, log service events, schedule upcoming service with due-date or mileage triggers. Driver-facing route interactions and sysadmin features are out of scope.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Route list layout
- Each route card shows: route name, status badge, stop count, assigned truck
- Routes grouped by status (Active, Scheduled, Completed) — collapsible sections
- Active routes appear first

### Route detail screen
- Header: assigned driver, truck, and route status (minimal — no dates or notes)
- Stops displayed as a vertical timeline — nodes/dots connected by a line, each showing address and status (pending/arrived/departed)

### Route create/edit form
- Same fields as the web form (name, driver, truck, multi-stop with AddressInput) but adapted for mobile UX — step-by-step or bottom-sheet style entry
- Required fields: name, driver, truck, and at least 2 stops
- Adding stops: "Add stop" button at bottom of the stop list
- Removing stops: swipe left on a stop to delete
- Full edit allowed on active routes — no restrictions once a route is in progress

### Maintenance service types
- Predefined list for service type selection (e.g. oil change, tire rotation, brake service, inspection, etc.) — no free text
- Mileage is manual numeric entry (owner types the odometer reading)

### Service history display
- Claude's discretion on layout (chronological list is the likely best fit for mobile — newest first, each event showing date, type, mileage)

### Maintenance navigation
- Two entry points: from the truck detail screen (maintenance section/tab) AND from a top-level maintenance area in the app filterable by truck

### Upcoming service scheduling
- A scheduled service can be triggered by due date, mileage threshold, or both — owner sets whichever applies
- Overdue/due-soon alerts surface in two places: warning badge/indicator on the truck entry in the truck list, and a "Due Soon" section at the top of the maintenance screen listing all pending items across trucks
- Scheduling UX: Claude's discretion — design the form based on what was decided above (date + mileage fields, separate from the past-event log form)
- Completion flow: Claude's discretion — pick the most intuitive resolution pattern

### Claude's Discretion
- Service history display layout (chronological list assumed, but Claude can choose)
- Upcoming service scheduling form UX (how it differs from the log-a-past-event form)
- Scheduled service completion flow (auto-resolve vs manual mark complete)
- Exact predefined service type list (build a sensible default set for trucking: oil change, tire rotation, brake inspection, DOT inspection, transmission service, coolant flush, filter replacement, etc.)

</decisions>

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- The route form on mobile should feel like a mobile-native experience, not a shrunk-down version of the web form. Step-by-step or bottom-sheet entry is preferred over a long scrolling form.
- The AddressInput component (built in Phase 127-02) should be used for stop entry.
- Swipe-to-delete on stop rows is the preferred mobile interaction pattern.

</specifics>
