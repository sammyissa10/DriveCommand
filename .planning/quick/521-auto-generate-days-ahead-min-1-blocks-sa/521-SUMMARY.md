## Report-Only Finding (not fixed, per plan)

The desktop **Auto Generate Days Ahead** field at `RouteTemplateForm.tsx:787-806` is rendered **unconditionally** — it is not gated on `scheduleType`. Both mobile variants (`TemplateEditMobile.tsx:307`, `NewTemplateMobile.tsx:243`) gate the equivalent row behind the recurrence-fields conditional, so the field is already hidden for `on_call` there.

This desktop/mobile inconsistency is the deeper reason the bug was reachable at all: on desktop an `on_call` template shows — and validates — a field that has no real meaning for it (there is no recurrence rule and no scheduled departure to count days ahead of). Hiding the field on desktop for `on_call` is the obvious follow-up, but it is a behavior change to a form shared by all schedule types and was explicitly out of scope for this task. Recorded here for a future quick task.
