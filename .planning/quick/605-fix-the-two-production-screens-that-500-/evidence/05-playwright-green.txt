--- THE FLAKE, CHARACTERISED RATHER THAN HIDDEN

At the config's default `workers: 3`, `/carrier/driver-pay/settlements` failed on 2 of
3 runs and passed on the third. The correlated server-log entry is NOT a nuqs error:

  ⨯ Error: timeout exceeded when trying to connect
      at async getTenantPrisma (src\lib\context\tenant-context.ts:179:3)
      at async SettlementsPage (src\app\(owner)\carrier\driver-pay\settlements\page.tsx:49:18)
   GET /carrier/driver-pay/settlements 500 in 5.6s

It is Postgres connection-pool exhaustion, which `playwright.config.ts` documents in its
own comment ("the withTenantRLS extension wraps every query in a transaction; too many
parallel tests saturate the Supabase connection pool"), amplified here by the staging
`app_user` string carrying `connection_limit=1`. Runs at `--workers=1`: 3 for 3 green.

Recorded rather than smoothed over: on a metered staging connection this spec is
concurrency-sensitive. In CI `workers` is already 1 (`process.env.CI ? 1 : 3`), so the
configuration CI actually uses is the one that was green here.
