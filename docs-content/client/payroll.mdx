# Payroll Management

<Callout variant="info">
DriveCommand payroll helps you track driver compensation with base pay, performance bonuses, deductions, and miles logged. Generate PDF payslips and export payroll data for accounting systems.
</Callout>

## Overview

Use payroll to:

- **Track driver earnings** — base pay, bonuses, commissions, and per-mile rates
- **Manage deductions** — insurance, advances, equipment fees
- **Calculate net pay** — gross pay minus deductions
- **Generate payslips** — professional PDF payslips for driver records
- **Export for accounting** — CSV export for QuickBooks or other systems

<PlanBadge tier="business" /> This feature requires a Business plan or higher.

## Create a payroll record

<StepFlow>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Open Payroll">
    Navigate to **Finance → Payroll** in the sidebar.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Click New Payroll Record">
    The payroll creation form opens.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Select driver and pay period">
    Choose the driver and enter the pay period start and end dates (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly).
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Enter base pay">
    Enter the base pay amount. This can be:
    - **Fixed salary** — flat amount per pay period
    - **Hourly rate** — hours worked × hourly rate
    - **Per-mile rate** — miles logged × rate per mile (auto-calculated from driver's routes)
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Add bonuses">
    Add performance bonuses or commissions:
    - Safety bonus
    - On-time delivery bonus
    - Referral bonus
    - Percentage of load revenue
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Add deductions">
    Enter deductions:
    - Health insurance
    - Fuel advances
    - Equipment lease fees
    - Other deductions
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Review and save">
    Verify gross pay, total deductions, and net pay. Click **Save Payroll Record**.
  </StepFlow.Step>
</StepFlow>

## Pay period management

DriveCommand supports flexible pay periods:

- **Weekly** — Sunday to Saturday
- **Bi-weekly** — every two weeks
- **Semi-monthly** — 1st-15th and 16th-end of month
- **Monthly** — full calendar month

Set the pay period start and end dates when creating a payroll record. The system auto-calculates miles logged during that period from the driver's completed loads.

## Miles logged auto-calculation

When you create a payroll record for a driver, DriveCommand automatically calculates miles logged:

1. Finds all loads completed by the driver during the pay period
2. Sums the loaded miles from each load
3. Populates the **Miles Logged** field
4. If pay is per-mile, calculates base pay: `miles × rate per mile`

You can override the auto-calculated miles if needed (e.g., for manual adjustments or deadhead miles).

## Bonuses and deductions

Add multiple bonuses and deductions to each payroll record:

**Bonuses:**
- Safety bonus (e.g., $100 for zero incidents in pay period)
- On-time delivery bonus
- Fuel efficiency bonus (based on MPG targets)
- Referral bonus (for bringing new drivers)
- Percentage of load revenue (commission-based pay)

**Deductions:**
- Health insurance premiums
- Fuel or cash advances issued during the period
- Equipment lease or rental fees
- Uniform or safety gear purchases
- Other deductions (garnishments, etc.)

Each bonus or deduction has a description field for payslip clarity.

## Net pay calculation

Net pay is calculated automatically:

```
Gross Pay = Base Pay + Total Bonuses
Net Pay = Gross Pay - Total Deductions
```

The payroll detail page shows the full breakdown with line-by-line amounts.

## Generate PDF payslip

Click **Download Payslip** on any payroll record to generate a PDF with:

- Driver name and address
- Pay period dates
- Base pay (with rate and hours or miles)
- Itemized bonuses
- Itemized deductions
- Gross pay, total deductions, and net pay
- Company name and contact info

Drivers can use payslips for loan applications or tax records.

## Export for accounting

Export payroll data to CSV for integration with QuickBooks, ADP, or other accounting systems:

1. Go to **Finance → Payroll**
2. Filter by date range or driver
3. Click **Export to CSV**
4. Import the CSV into your accounting software

CSV includes: driver name, pay period, base pay, bonuses, deductions, gross pay, net pay.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Pro Tip">

## Frequently asked questions

**Can drivers view their payroll records?**

Not yet. Driver payroll visibility is planned for a future mobile app release. For now, email PDF payslips to drivers.

**Does DriveCommand calculate payroll taxes?**

No. DriveCommand tracks gross and net pay but does not calculate federal, state, or local taxes. Consult your accountant or use a payroll service like ADP or Gusto for tax compliance.

**Can I backdate payroll records?**

Yes. You can create payroll records for any historical pay period. Enter the pay period dates when creating the record.
