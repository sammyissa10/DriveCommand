# Hours of Service

<Callout variant="info">
Hours of Service (HOS) logging helps drivers track their duty status and remaining hours under FMCSA regulations. The system automatically calculates 14-hour and 11-hour limits and warns drivers when they're approaching HOS violations.
</Callout>

## Overview

Drivers use the HOS screen to:

- Log duty status changes (On Duty, Driving, Sleeper Berth, Off Duty)
- View remaining hours in the current 14-hour window
- See remaining drive time (11-hour limit)
- Review the current day's status timeline

<PlanBadge tier="pro" /> This feature requires a Pro plan or higher.

## FMCSA limits

DriveCommand enforces two key FMCSA rules:

1. **14-hour on-duty limit** — drivers cannot drive after 14 hours of on-duty time, even if they have drive time remaining
2. **11-hour driving limit** — drivers can drive for a maximum of 11 hours within the 14-hour window

The HOS screen shows countdown timers for both limits.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Compliance Notice">
Drivers must take a minimum 10-hour break before starting a new 14-hour duty period. Attempting to go On Duty before the 10-hour break completes will trigger a violation warning.
</Callout>

## Log a status change

<StepFlow>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Open the HOS screen">
    Tap **Hours** in the driver dashboard or bottom navigation (mobile app).
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Select your new status">
    The status grid shows four options:
    - **Off Duty** — not working, not driving
    - **Sleeper Berth** — rest period in sleeper berth (counts toward 10-hour break)
    - **On Duty (Not Driving)** — working but not behind the wheel (pre-trip, loading, fueling)
    - **Driving** — actively driving
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Confirm the change">
    Tap the status card. A confirmation modal appears showing:
    - Old status → New status
    - Timestamp of the change
    - Remaining hours after this change
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Submit">
    Tap **Confirm**. The status change is logged immediately and cannot be edited.
  </StepFlow.Step>
</StepFlow>

## HOS screen layout

The HOS screen shows:

### Status grid (2x2)

Four tappable cards with current status highlighted in green. Tap any card to change status.

### Day bar

Visual timeline showing the current day's status changes with color-coded segments:
- **Green** — Off Duty or Sleeper Berth
- **Yellow** — On Duty (Not Driving)
- **Red** — Driving

### Countdown clocks

Two circular timers:
- **14-hour clock** — hours remaining in the on-duty window (turns red at < 1 hour)
- **11-hour clock** — hours remaining for driving (turns red at < 30 minutes)

### Violation warnings

If a driver attempts to exceed limits, a red banner appears:

- **HOS violation imminent** — less than 30 minutes remaining
- **HOS violation** — limit reached, cannot continue driving
- **Break required** — 10-hour break not completed

<Callout variant="danger" title="Critical: HOS Violations">
Driving after HOS limits are reached is a federal violation and can result in fines, out-of-service orders, and CSA points. Ensure drivers complete their 10-hour breaks before starting new duty periods.
</Callout>

## ELD integration

If your fleet uses an integrated ELD (Samsara, Motive/KeepTruckin), HOS data syncs automatically:

- Status changes from the ELD appear in DriveCommand
- Status changes logged in DriveCommand push to the ELD

To enable ELD integration, navigate to **Settings → Integrations** and connect your ELD provider.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I edit a past HOS entry?**

No. HOS entries are immutable once logged to maintain compliance records. If you need to correct an error, contact support.

**What happens if I forget to log a status change?**

Your last logged status remains active until you log a new one. If you forgot to go Off Duty at the end of your shift, your on-duty hours will continue accruing. Log the correct status as soon as you remember.

**Does the system automatically change my status to Driving when the truck moves?**

Not yet. GPS-based auto-status is planned for a future release. Currently, drivers must manually log all status changes.
