# Driver Documents

<Callout variant="info">
Driver Documents is your digital filing cabinet. Upload compliance documents, track expiration dates, and get alerts before certifications expire — all from your phone.
</Callout>

## Overview

The Documents screen shows all your uploaded compliance files:

- **Driver's License** — CDL, Class A/B/C
- **Medical Card** — DOT physical exam certificate
- **Endorsements** — Hazmat, tanker, doubles/triples
- **Certifications** — Twic card, port access, customer-specific badges
- **Insurance** — Proof of insurance (if required)

Each document shows:

- **Document type**
- **Expiration date** (if applicable)
- **Upload date**
- **Status indicator** (valid, expiring soon, expired)

<PlanBadge tier="pro" /> This feature requires a Pro plan or higher.

## Upload a document

<StepFlow>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Open Documents">
    Navigate to **More → Documents**.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Tap Upload">
    Tap the blue **+** (plus) button at the bottom right.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Select document type">
    Choose from the dropdown:
    - Driver's License
    - Medical Card
    - Hazmat Endorsement
    - Other Certification
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Take or select photo">
    Options:
    - **Take Photo** — Capture with your camera (recommended for clear images)
    - **Choose from Library** — Upload an existing photo
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Enter expiration date">
    For documents that expire (license, medical card, endorsements), select the expiration date from the date picker.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Upload">
    Tap **Upload Document**. You'll see a progress bar as the file uploads. When complete, the document appears in your list.
  </StepFlow.Step>
</StepFlow>

<Callout variant="tip" title="Photo tips">
- Use good lighting (avoid shadows and glare)
- Capture the entire document (all four corners visible)
- Ensure text is readable (check focus before uploading)
- Hold your phone directly above the document (no angles)

Clear photos prevent rejection and re-upload requests from dispatch.
</Callout>

## Document status indicators

Each document has a colored dot indicator:

- **Green dot** — Valid (more than 30 days until expiration)
- **Yellow dot** — Expiring soon (less than 30 days remaining)
- **Red dot** — Expired

Your fleet manager sees the same indicators on their compliance dashboard.

## Expiration alerts

You'll receive push notifications at:

- **30 days before expiration** — "Your medical card expires in 30 days"
- **14 days before expiration** — Second reminder
- **7 days before expiration** — Final warning
- **Day of expiration** — "Your medical card expired today"

<Callout variant="danger" title="Driving with expired documents">
Operating a CMV with an expired license or medical card is a federal violation. You can be placed out-of-service immediately and may face fines. Upload renewed documents before the expiration date.
</Callout>

## View a document

Tap any document in the list to open the detail view. You'll see:

- **Full-size image** — Zoom and pan to read details
- **Document info** — Type, upload date, expiration date
- **Download option** — Save a copy to your phone

The document opens in your phone's default image viewer.

## Replace a document

To upload a renewed version:

1. Upload the new document using the same document type
2. The system automatically archives the old version
3. Your compliance status updates immediately

Old versions are retained for audit history but marked as "Superseded."

## Document storage

Documents are stored securely in encrypted cloud storage (Cloudflare R2). Access is restricted to:

- You (the driver)
- Your fleet manager
- SysAdmin (for compliance audits only)

Documents are never shared with third parties without your explicit consent.

## Compliance dashboard

Your fleet manager monitors document compliance across the entire fleet. When your documents are up-to-date:

- Your driver profile shows a green compliance dot
- You remain eligible for load assignments
- No compliance holds are placed on your account

## Frequently asked questions

**What file formats are supported?**

Images only: JPG, PNG, HEIC (iPhone photos), WebP. Maximum file size: 10 MB. If you have a PDF, take a screenshot of it and upload the image.

**Can I delete a document?**

No. Once uploaded, documents are permanent (even archived versions). This maintains audit integrity. If you uploaded the wrong file, upload the correct one to replace it.

**What if my document doesn't have an expiration date?**

Leave the expiration date field blank. Some certifications (like training certificates) don't expire.

**Why was my document rejected?**

Common reasons:
- Image too blurry or dark
- Document cropped (corners cut off)
- Wrong document type uploaded
- Expiration date doesn't match the image

Check your notifications for the specific rejection reason, then re-upload.
