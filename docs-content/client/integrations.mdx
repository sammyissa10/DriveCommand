# Third-Party Integrations

<Callout variant="info">
DriveCommand integrates with popular trucking and business software to eliminate manual data entry. Connect accounting systems, ELD providers, factoring services, and email platforms for seamless data flow.
</Callout>

## Overview

Use Integrations to:

- **Sync accounting data** — export invoices and expenses to QuickBooks or Xero
- **Import GPS and HOS data** — connect Samsara or Motive/KeepTruckin for automatic GPS tracking
- **Automate factoring** — send invoices directly to factoring companies for faster payment
- **Email from your domain** — use custom SMTP for branded email notifications
- **Reduce manual entry** — data flows automatically between systems

<PlanBadge tier="business" /> This feature requires a Business plan or higher.

## QuickBooks Online integration

Sync invoices, expenses, and customers with QuickBooks Online.

### Setup

<StepFlow>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Open Integrations">
    Navigate to **Settings → Integrations** in the sidebar.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Click Connect next to QuickBooks Online">
    You'll be redirected to Intuit's authorization page.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Log in to QuickBooks">
    Sign in with your QuickBooks account credentials.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Authorize DriveCommand">
    Grant DriveCommand permission to read and write invoices, expenses, and customers.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Return to DriveCommand">
    You'll be redirected back to DriveCommand. The integration is now active.
  </StepFlow.Step>
</StepFlow>

### What syncs

Once connected, DriveCommand syncs:

- **Invoices** → QuickBooks Invoices (synced when invoice is marked "Sent" or "Paid")
- **Customers** → QuickBooks Customers (synced when customer is created in CRM)
- **Expenses** → QuickBooks Expenses (synced daily at midnight)

**Sync direction:** DriveCommand → QuickBooks (one-way). Changes made in QuickBooks are not synced back to DriveCommand.

**Sync frequency:** Invoices sync immediately, customers sync hourly, expenses sync daily.

### Troubleshooting

**Issue: Invoice not appearing in QuickBooks**

Check:
1. Invoice status is "Sent" or "Paid" (draft invoices are not synced)
2. Integration is connected (green checkmark on Integrations page)
3. Check error log on Integrations page for sync failures

**Issue: Duplicate customers in QuickBooks**

DriveCommand matches customers by email address. If a customer with the same email exists in QuickBooks, it updates the existing record instead of creating a duplicate. Ensure email addresses are consistent.

## Samsara ELD integration

Import GPS tracking data and driver HOS logs from Samsara.

### Setup

<StepFlow>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Get Samsara API key">
    Log in to Samsara dashboard → Settings → API Tokens → Create New Token.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Open Integrations in DriveCommand">
    Navigate to **Settings → Integrations** → Samsara section.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Enter API key">
    Paste your Samsara API token into the API Key field.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Click Connect">
    DriveCommand validates the token and fetches your fleet list.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Map trucks">
    Match Samsara vehicles to DriveCommand trucks by VIN or name.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Save mapping">
    Click **Save**. GPS and HOS data will sync automatically.
  </StepFlow.Step>
</StepFlow>

### What syncs

Once connected, DriveCommand imports:

- **GPS reports** — truck location pings (every 5 minutes) for live map and IFTA reporting
- **HOS entries** — driver duty status changes (driving, on-duty, off-duty, sleeper berth)
- **Vehicle diagnostics** — odometer, fuel level, engine hours (displayed on truck detail page)

**Sync frequency:** GPS every 5 minutes, HOS every 15 minutes, diagnostics hourly.

**Data retention:** GPS and HOS data is stored for 2 years to support IFTA reporting and compliance.

## Motive (KeepTruckin) integration

## Factoring integration (OTR Capital example)

Send invoices to your factoring company for immediate payment.

### Setup

<StepFlow>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Open Integrations">
    Navigate to **Settings → Integrations** → Factoring section.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Select factoring provider">
    Choose OTR Capital (or your provider from the dropdown).
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Enter factoring account ID">
    Enter your OTR Capital account number or customer ID.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Authorize connection">
    Click **Connect**. You may be redirected to OTR Capital to authorize the connection.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Configure auto-submit">
    Choose whether invoices are submitted automatically when marked "Sent" or require manual approval.
  </StepFlow.Step>
</StepFlow>

### How it works

1. Create an invoice in DriveCommand
2. Mark invoice as "Sent"
3. DriveCommand submits invoice to factoring company via API
4. Factoring company advances payment (typically 80-95% within 24 hours)
5. When factoring company receives payment from customer, they remit the balance to you

Factoring status is displayed on the invoice detail page (Submitted, Funded, Completed).

## Custom SMTP email integration

Use your own email server for branded notifications.

### Setup

1. Go to **Settings → Integrations** → Email section
2. Enter SMTP settings:
   - **SMTP Host** (e.g., smtp.gmail.com)
   - **SMTP Port** (587 for TLS, 465 for SSL)
   - **Username** (your email address)
   - **Password** (app password or SMTP password)
   - **From Name** (your company name)
   - **From Email** (your email address)
3. Click **Test Connection** to verify settings
4. Click **Save**

All DriveCommand notification emails (driver invitations, expiry reminders, etc.) will now send from your email address instead of noreply@drivecommand.com.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Pro Tip">
Use an app-specific password for Gmail SMTP instead of your main password. Generate an app password in Google Account Settings → Security → App Passwords.
</Callout>

## Disconnect integrations

To disconnect an integration:

1. Go to **Settings → Integrations**
2. Find the connected integration
3. Click **Disconnect**
4. Confirm the action

**Data impact:** Disconnecting does not delete synced data. Historical data (invoices, GPS reports) remains in DriveCommand. Future syncs will stop.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I connect multiple QuickBooks accounts?**

No. DriveCommand supports one QuickBooks connection per account. If you have multiple QuickBooks companies, choose the primary one for sync.

**What happens if an invoice sync fails?**

Failed syncs are logged on the Integrations page. Click **View Error Log** to see the error message. Common issues: missing customer in QuickBooks, invalid account mapping. Retry the sync after fixing the issue.

**Can I sync data from QuickBooks to DriveCommand?**

Not yet. Sync is currently one-way (DriveCommand → QuickBooks). Two-way sync is planned for a future release.

**Do integrations cost extra?**

No. Integrations are included in the Business and Enterprise plans at no additional cost. You may incur costs from the third-party service (e.g., QuickBooks subscription, Samsara API fees).
