# Public Shipment Tracking

<Callout variant="info">
Give your customers visibility into their shipments with shareable tracking links. No login required.
</Callout>

## Overview

Public Shipment Tracking allows you to share a unique tracking link with customers so they can see:

- **Live GPS location** — Real-time truck position on a map
- **Status timeline** — Pickup, in-transit, delivery milestones
- **Estimated arrival** — ETA based on current location and route
- **Contact info** — Driver name and phone number (if enabled)

Customers access the tracking page without needing to create an account or log in.

## Generating a Tracking Link

Every load automatically gets a unique tracking token when created. Here's how to share it with customers:

<StepFlow>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Open Load Details">
    Navigate to **Loads** and click on the load you want to share.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Find the Tracking Link">
    In the load detail panel, you'll see a "Customer Tracking" section with a unique URL like `drivecommand.com/track/abc123xyz`.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Share the Link">
    Click "Copy Link" and send it to your customer via email, SMS, or your load confirmation. The link works immediately and stays active until the load is delivered.
  </StepFlow.Step>
</StepFlow>

<Callout variant="tip" title="Pro Tip">
Add the tracking link to your automated load confirmation emails so customers get instant visibility without having to ask.
</Callout>

## What Customers See

When a customer clicks the tracking link, they see:

- **Interactive map** — Real-time truck position with route polyline
- **Shipment details** — Load number, pickup/delivery addresses, commodity
- **Status timeline** — Visual progress indicator showing completed and upcoming stops
- **ETA countdown** — Hours remaining until delivery
- **Driver contact** — Name and phone number (if you've enabled sharing)

## Privacy & Security

<Callout variant="success">
Tracking links are secure and only show information relevant to the specific load. Customers cannot see your other loads or fleet data.
</Callout>

**What's visible:**
- Load-specific GPS updates (refreshed every 5 minutes)
- Pickup and delivery addresses
- Commodity and reference numbers (BOL, PO)
- Driver name and phone (if enabled in settings)

**What's NOT visible:**
- Other loads or routes
- Truck details (VIN, registration)
- Your company financials or invoices
- Driver personal information (beyond name/phone)

## Disabling Driver Contact Sharing

By default, customers can see the driver's name and phone number. To disable this:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Privacy**
2. Toggle **"Share driver contact on public tracking"** to OFF
3. Future tracking links will hide driver contact info

This applies to all new tracking links. Existing links are not affected retroactively.

## Tracking Link Expiry

Tracking links automatically expire after the load is marked as **DELIVERED** or **CANCELLED**. Customers will see:

> "This shipment has been delivered. Tracking is no longer available."

If you need to extend tracking after delivery (e.g., for proof of delivery), contact support.

## Mobile-Friendly Design

The tracking page is fully responsive and optimized for mobile devices. Customers can check shipment status from their phone, tablet, or desktop without any app installation.

## Customization

<PlanBadge tier="business" /> Business plan customers can customize the tracking page:

- **Company logo** — Display your logo at the top of the tracking page
- **Brand colors** — Match the page to your company branding
- **Custom domain** — Use `tracking.yourcompany.com` instead of `drivecommand.com/track`

Contact support to enable these customization options.

## GPS Update Frequency

GPS updates are refreshed every:

- **5 minutes** — When driver is on an active route
- **10 minutes** — When driver is off-duty but load is in-transit
- **No updates** — When load status is PENDING or DELIVERED

<Callout variant="warning" title="GPS Accuracy">
GPS accuracy depends on the driver's device signal strength. In areas with poor cell coverage, updates may be delayed or less accurate.
</Callout>

## Common Questions

**Can I regenerate a tracking link?**

No. Each load has a permanent tracking token that cannot be changed. If you need to revoke access, mark the load as CANCELLED and create a new load.

**Do customers need to refresh the page?**

No. The tracking page auto-refreshes GPS position every 60 seconds while the page is open.

**Can I see who viewed the tracking link?**

Not currently. This feature is planned for a future release.
