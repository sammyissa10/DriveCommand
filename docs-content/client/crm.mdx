# Customer Relationship Management

<Callout variant="info">
CRM provides customer and contact management for shippers, brokers, and consignees. Track load history, revenue totals, communication logs, and relationship health to grow your business.
</Callout>

## Overview

The CRM system helps you manage business relationships with:

- **Customers** — companies you haul freight for (shippers, brokers, 3PLs)
- **Contacts** — individual people at those companies
- **Interactions** — logged calls, emails, and meetings
- **Load history** — all shipments for each customer
- **Revenue analytics** — total revenue and trends per customer

<PlanBadge tier="pro" /> This feature requires a Pro plan or higher.

## Add a customer

<StepFlow>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Navigate to CRM">
    In the sidebar, click **CRM** under Legacy Features.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Click New Customer">
    The customer creation form appears.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Enter company details">
    Required fields:
    - **Company name** (e.g., "ABC Logistics")
    - **Customer type** — Shipper, Broker, 3PL, or Consignee

    Optional fields:
    - **MC number** — FMCSA motor carrier number
    - **DOT number** — for carrier customers
    - **Address** — company headquarters
    - **Website**
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Add billing information (optional)">
    - **Payment terms** — Net 15, Net 30, Net 60, etc.
    - **Credit limit**
    - **Factoring preference** — if customer requires factoring
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Save the customer">
    Click **Save**. The customer appears in your CRM list.
  </StepFlow.Step>
</StepFlow>

## Add contacts to a customer

Each customer can have multiple contacts (sales reps, dispatchers, billing departments):

1. Open the customer detail page
2. Scroll to **Contacts** section
3. Click **Add Contact**
4. Enter:
   - **Name**
   - **Role** (e.g., "Dispatcher", "Accounts Payable", "Sales Manager")
   - **Email and phone number**
   - **Primary contact** checkbox (one per customer)
5. Click **Save**

Primary contacts appear first in autocomplete dropdowns when creating loads.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Pro Tip">
Add multiple contacts for different purposes: one for dispatch, one for billing, one for sales. This ensures you always reach the right person.
</Callout>

## Log customer interactions

Track every conversation, email, and meeting to maintain relationship continuity:

<StepFlow>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Open customer detail page">
    Click the customer in the CRM list.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Scroll to Interactions section">
    Click **Log Interaction**.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Select interaction type">
    Choose from:
    - **Phone call**
    - **Email**
    - **In-person meeting**
    - **Note** (general entry)
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Add details">
    - **Subject** — brief summary (e.g., "Rate negotiation call")
    - **Notes** — detailed description of conversation
    - **Date and time**
    - **Contact** — which contact you spoke with
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Save the interaction">
    Click **Save**. The interaction appears in the timeline with newest first.
  </StepFlow.Step>
</StepFlow>

Use interaction logs to prepare for follow-up calls and ensure your team knows the relationship history.

## View load history

The customer detail page shows all loads for that customer:

- **Total loads** — lifetime count
- **Active loads** — currently IN_TRANSIT or AT_PICKUP
- **Delivered loads** — completed shipments
- **Load timeline** — chronological list with dates, routes, and revenue

Click any load in the timeline to view full load details.

## Track revenue analytics

The CRM dashboard shows revenue metrics per customer:

- **Total revenue** — all-time earnings from this customer
- **Last 30 days** — recent revenue
- **Average load value** — typical shipment size
- **Payment status** — outstanding invoices vs. paid

Use this data to identify your best customers and prioritize relationship-building efforts.

<Callout variant="success">
Sort the customer list by "Total Revenue" to see your top customers at a glance. Focus retention efforts on high-value accounts.
</Callout>

## Set payment terms

Configure payment expectations for each customer:

1. Open customer detail page
2. Click **Edit Customer**
3. Set **Payment Terms** (Net 15, Net 30, etc.)
4. Set **Credit Limit** (optional)
5. Click **Save**

When creating invoices, the system auto-applies the customer's payment terms and warns you if the invoice exceeds their credit limit.

## Manage customer tags

Organize customers with tags for filtering and grouping:

1. Open customer detail page
2. Scroll to **Tags** section
3. Click **Add Tag**
4. Select an existing tag or create a new one
5. Click **Apply**

Example tags: "Preferred Customer", "High Volume", "Requires Factoring", "Net 30 Terms"

Filter the CRM list by tags to quickly find customer segments.

## Frequently asked questions

**What's the difference between CRM and Carrier Clients?**

CRM is the original customer management interface. Carrier Clients is the newer interface with advanced filtering, contract management, and rate tables. Both access the same customer data.

**Can I import customers from a spreadsheet?**

Not yet. Customers must be added individually through the web interface. Bulk import is planned for a future release.

**How do I merge duplicate customers?**

Contact support via the Support Ticket system. Merging customers requires database-level changes to preserve load history and prevent data loss.

**Can customers log in to see their load status?**

No. Customers don't have login access. Instead, generate public tracking links for each load and share those URLs with your customers.
