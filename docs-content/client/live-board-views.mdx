# Reading the Live Board

<Callout variant="info">
The map answers *where is everyone*. The board answers *how is everyone doing* — which is the question you actually have at nine in the morning. It is the same information, listed instead of pinned.
</Callout>

## Getting there

In the left-hand menu, under **Intelligence**, choose **Live Board**. If you are already on the Live Map, the **List** control switches you across without losing your filters.

## The two views

At the top of the list you can switch between **Drivers** and **Trucks**. It is the same day either way — only what each row leads with, and what it thinks is worth telling you, changes.

Use **Drivers** when you are working the day. Use **Trucks** when you are asking which vehicles are tied up, or what is coming due.

<Callout variant="warning">
The **Next due** column on the Trucks view is about paperwork — licence, registration, insurance — and it says which one it is showing. It is not a service schedule. If nothing is on file it says **Nothing on file** rather than implying everything is fine.
</Callout>

## Reading a row

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Planned** / **In Progress** | Whether the driver has started |
| **Current or next stop** | Where they are, or where they are heading, from what they have marked done |
| **Stops** | How far through the run they are — 4 / 12 |
| **Window closes** | When the appointment at the next stop runs out |
| Inspection | Whether a pre-trip walkaround was needed, and how it went |
| On time | Whether they are running to their appointment times |
| **GPS** | How recently the truck reported its position |

<Callout variant="tip">
Every badge on this screen is a colour **and** a symbol **and** a word. Cover the colour with your thumb and it still reads — which matters on a bright screen in a yard, and matters to anyone who does not see red and green as different.
</Callout>

## Two things it will not pretend to know

- **Where the truck is** comes from the stops the driver has marked, not from a guess. When there is nothing to go on it says so rather than naming a town it is not sure about. The GPS age is a separate figure so it is never mistaken for position.
- **Window closes** is the appointment time, not an estimated arrival. Nothing here is predicting when the driver will get there.

## When the board cannot load

If the vehicle positions cannot be fetched, the board says so. It will not show you an empty list as though your fleet were idle — an empty list means empty, and a failure says failure.
