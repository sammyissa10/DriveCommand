# Subscription Management

<Callout variant="info">
DriveCommand offers flexible subscription plans to match your fleet size and operational needs. Upgrade for advanced features, downgrade to save costs, or manage billing settings.
</Callout>

## Overview

Use Subscription Management to:

- **View current plan** — see your active plan tier and included features
- **Upgrade or downgrade** — change plans to match your needs
- **View billing history** — see past invoices and payment dates
- **Update payment method** — change credit card or billing address
- **Cancel subscription** — pause or cancel your account

<PlanBadge tier="free" /> All DriveCommand accounts start with a Free plan.

## Subscription plans

DriveCommand offers four plan tiers:

**Free plan** includes basic load and driver management for up to 2 trucks and 3 drivers. Limited features.

**Starter plan** unlocks invoicing, compliance tracking, and fleet messaging for small fleets.

**Pro plan** adds IFTA reporting, profit predictor, live GPS map, and checklists/workflows.

**Business plan** includes workflow automation, lane analytics, AI document reader, and priority support.

**Enterprise plan** offers custom integrations, API access, white-label options, and dedicated account manager.

## Upgrade your plan

<StepFlow>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Open Subscription">
    Navigate to **Settings → Subscription** in the sidebar.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Click Upgrade Plan">
    The plan selection page opens.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Choose a plan tier">
    Select Starter, Pro, Business, or Enterprise. Compare features and pricing.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Enter payment details">
    Enter credit card information and billing address.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Confirm upgrade">
    Click **Confirm Upgrade**. Your plan changes immediately and you're billed on the next billing cycle.
  </StepFlow.Step>
</StepFlow>

**Upgrade billing:** When you upgrade, you're charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the current billing cycle. The full new plan price applies starting next month.

**Immediate access:** Upgraded features are available immediately after confirmation.

## Downgrade your plan

To downgrade to a lower tier:

1. Go to **Settings → Subscription**
2. Click **Change Plan**
3. Select a lower-tier plan
4. Review the feature restrictions (you may lose access to certain features)
5. Click **Confirm Downgrade**

**Downgrade billing:** Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing cycle. You retain access to your current plan features until then. No refunds for partial months.

**Feature restrictions:** If you downgrade and lose access to features (e.g., workflows, IFTA reporting), your data is retained but inaccessible. If you re-upgrade, all data is restored.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Important">
Downgrading may disable features you're actively using. Review the feature comparison before confirming the downgrade.
</Callout>

## View billing history

The Subscription page includes a **Billing History** section with:

- Invoice date
- Invoice number
- Amount paid
- Payment method (last 4 digits of card)
- Invoice status (Paid, Pending, Overdue)
- Download PDF link

Click **Download PDF** to save a copy of any invoice for your records.

## Update payment method

To change your credit card or billing address:

1. Go to **Settings → Subscription**
2. Click **Update Payment Method**
3. Enter new credit card details and billing address
4. Click **Save**

The new payment method will be used for the next billing cycle.

## Cancel subscription

To cancel your DriveCommand subscription:

1. Go to **Settings → Subscription**
2. Click **Cancel Subscription**
3. Select a reason for canceling (optional feedback)
4. Choose:
   - **Cancel at end of billing cycle** — retain access until your current billing period ends
   - **Cancel immediately** — lose access immediately, no refund for partial month
5. Confirm cancellation

**Data retention:** After canceling, your data is retained for 90 days in case you reactivate. After 90 days, all data is permanently deleted.

**Reactivation:** To reactivate a canceled account, log in and select a new plan on the Subscription page.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Pro Tip">
If you're canceling due to cost, consider downgrading to the Starter plan instead. You'll retain access to core features and keep your data active.
</Callout>

## Trial period

New DriveCommand accounts include a 14-day free trial of the Pro plan. During the trial:

- All Pro features are unlocked
- No credit card required to start
- You can upgrade to a paid plan anytime during the trial
- If you don't upgrade by the end of the trial, you're automatically downgraded to the Free plan

Trial reminder emails are sent 7 days, 3 days, and 1 day before the trial ends.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I switch between annual and monthly billing?**

Yes. Annual billing (paid yearly) offers a 20% discount compared to monthly billing. Switch on the Subscription page.

**What happens if my payment fails?**

If a payment fails, we'll retry 3 times over 7 days. If all retries fail, your account is suspended. You'll receive email notifications at each step. Update your payment method to reactivate.

**Do I get a refund if I cancel mid-month?**

No. DriveCommand does not offer refunds for partial months. If you cancel mid-cycle, you retain access until the end of the current billing period.

**Can I export my data before canceling?**

Yes. Export loads, invoices, drivers, and trucks to CSV from their respective list pages. Exports are available on all plan tiers, including Free.
