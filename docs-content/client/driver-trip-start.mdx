# Starting a trip and doing the walkaround

<Callout variant="info">
Short version: open your trip, press **Start Trip**. If your company asks for a pre-trip inspection you will be sent to walk the truck first. Answer everything honestly — a fail is not a problem you are causing, it is a problem you are reporting.
</Callout>

## Starting a trip

Your trip is on your home screen. Press **Start Trip & Navigate**.

If no inspection is needed you are moving straight away. If one is, you will be told, and the way to it is right there.

## Doing the walkaround

You can also start it before you press Start Trip — there is a **Pre-trip inspection** link on your trip card. Most drivers find it easier to walk the truck first and start the trip afterwards.

<StepFlow>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Press Start the walkaround">
    The opening screen names the unit you are inspecting. **Not now** takes you back if you have got the wrong truck.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Work through the sections">
    One section at a time — the top of the screen says which, for example *Section 3 of 6*, and how many items you have answered. Each item has three answers: **Pass**, **Fail**, and **N/A** for something this truck does not have.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Describe anything you fail">
    A fail needs a note, and it will tell you how many more characters it wants. Write what a mechanic needs to know — the screen says as much: *this is what the mechanic will read.* Some items also ask for a photo, and it uploads there and then.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Go back and check">
    **Back to the checklist** works at any point before you sign. Nothing is final until you sign.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Sign and submit">
    The sign screen lists anything you flagged. Most checklists ask you to sign with your finger; some ask only for your printed name, and say so. Either way you enter your name, and then press **Sign and submit**.
  </StepFlow.Step>
</StepFlow>

<Callout variant="tip">
**You need a signal.** Answers, photos and your signature all go to the office as you make them — there is no offline mode for the walkaround yet. If a photo will not upload you will be told so plainly, and the note you wrote still counts.
</Callout>

<Callout variant="warning">
**Do not press Pass to get moving.** What you record here is the record of the truck's condition on the day, with your name on it. If you are not sure, fail it and write what you saw.
</Callout>

## What happens after you sign

| What you found | What happens |
|---|---|
| Everything passed | The trip starts. |
| Something failed, none of it critical | The trip starts, and what you flagged is logged against the truck for the shop. |
| Something critical failed | The trip is blocked. |

## If the trip is blocked

You will get a screen headed **This trip cannot start**, listing exactly what failed, with your notes and any photo you took.

You cannot clear it yourself — that takes an override from the office or a sign-off from the shop. What you can do is on the screen:

- **Contact dispatch** — opens messages. Do this rather than waiting; the system alerts dispatch automatically when a trip is blocked, but a message from you is faster and says more.
- **Check again** — re-reads the situation. Use it after the office tells you it is sorted, and you will not have to ring back to find out.

## Already inspected this truck today

If you inspected the same truck within the last twenty-four hours, you are not asked again — you will see something like *you inspected this truck 3 hours ago, no new walkaround needed*, and the trip starts. This is by the clock, not by the calendar date, so a late-night inspection still covers you after midnight.
