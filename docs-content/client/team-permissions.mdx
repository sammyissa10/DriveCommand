# Team Permissions

<Callout variant="info">
Team Permissions give you fine-grained control over who can access what in DriveCommand. Assign roles, configure permissions, and manage team members with secure invitation workflows.
</Callout>

## Overview

Use Team Permissions to:

- **Control access** — define who can view, create, edit, or delete loads, trucks, drivers, and invoices
- **Assign roles** — owner, manager, dispatcher, or driver roles with default permissions
- **Customize permissions** — override defaults to match your operational structure
- **Invite team members** — send secure email invitations with pre-configured permissions
- **Audit activity** — track who made changes and when

<PlanBadge tier="business" /> This feature requires a Business plan or higher.

## User roles

DriveCommand supports four primary roles:

<ComparisonTable
  headers={["Role", "Primary Use Case", "Default Permissions"]}
  rows={[
    ["Owner", "Company owner or administrator", "Full access to all features and settings"],
    ["Manager", "Operations manager or supervisor", "Manage loads, routes, drivers, trucks; cannot change billing or subscription"],
    ["Dispatcher", "Dispatch coordinator", "Create and assign loads, view routes, message drivers; read-only on trucks and invoices"],
    ["Driver", "Truck driver", "View assigned loads, update load status, upload documents, log HOS"]
  ]}
/>

**Owner role** has unrestricted access and cannot be edited. At least one owner must exist for each account.

**Manager, Dispatcher, and Driver roles** can be customized with granular permissions.

## Granular permissions

Each role can be configured with specific permissions across feature areas:

**Dispatch permissions:**
- View loads
- Create loads
- Edit loads
- Delete loads
- Assign drivers to loads
- Change load status
- View routes
- Create routes
- Edit routes

**Fleet permissions:**
- View drivers
- Invite drivers
- Edit driver profiles
- View trucks
- Create trucks
- Edit trucks
- Delete trucks

**Finance permissions:**
- View invoices
- Create invoices
- Edit invoices
- Delete invoices
- Mark invoices as paid
- View payroll
- Create payroll records

**Compliance permissions:**
- View compliance dashboard
- Upload documents
- Manage IFTA reports

**Settings permissions:**
- Manage team members
- Change subscription
- Configure integrations

Permissions are checkboxes on the team member edit page. Check to grant access, uncheck to deny.

## Invite a team member

<StepFlow>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Open Team Permissions">
    Navigate to **Settings → Team Permissions** in the sidebar.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Click Invite Team Member">
    The invitation form opens.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Enter email and name">
    Enter the new team member's email address and full name.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Select role">
    Choose the role: Manager, Dispatcher, or Driver. (Owner invitations are handled differently.)
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Configure permissions">
    Review and adjust permissions for this team member. Default permissions are pre-selected based on the role.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Send invitation">
    Click **Send Invitation**. The team member receives an email with a secure link to accept the invitation and set their password.
  </StepFlow.Step>
</StepFlow>

**Invitation expiry:** Invitation links expire after 7 days. If the team member doesn't accept within 7 days, resend the invitation.

## Accept invitation workflow

When a team member receives an invitation:

1. They click the link in the email
2. The invitation acceptance page loads
3. They enter a password and confirm it
4. They click **Accept Invitation**
5. Their account is created and they are logged in
6. They see the DriveCommand portal based on their role (owner/manager/dispatcher → web portal, driver → mobile app prompt)

<Callout variant="tip" title="Pro Tip">
Set permissions before sending the invitation. This ensures the team member has the correct access from day one. You can always adjust permissions later.
</Callout>

## Edit team member permissions

To change permissions for an existing team member:

1. Go to **Settings → Team Permissions**
2. Find the team member in the list
3. Click **Edit**
4. Check or uncheck permissions
5. Click **Save**

Permission changes take effect immediately. If the team member is logged in, they may need to refresh the page to see updated access.

## Remove team member access

To revoke access:

1. Go to **Settings → Team Permissions**
2. Find the team member in the list
3. Click **Remove** (or **Suspend** to temporarily disable access)
4. Confirm the action

**Removed vs. Suspended:**
- **Removed** — team member is permanently deleted, cannot log in
- **Suspended** — team member account is disabled but not deleted, can be reactivated later

Removed team members' historical actions (loads created, invoices issued) remain in the system with their name attached for audit purposes.

## Manager role examples

**Use case 1: Operations manager**

Permissions:
- Create/edit loads, routes, drivers, trucks
- View invoices (read-only)
- Manage team members
- Cannot change subscription or billing settings

**Use case 2: Compliance manager**

Permissions:
- View drivers and trucks
- Upload documents
- View compliance dashboard
- Manage IFTA reports
- Cannot create loads or invoices

**Use case 3: Safety manager**

Permissions:
- View drivers
- View incidents
- View safety analytics
- Cannot edit loads or routes

Customize the Manager role to match your operational structure.

## Driver invitations

Driver invitations work the same as other team member invitations, but with driver-specific defaults:

- **Default role:** Driver
- **Default permissions:** View my loads, update load status, upload documents, log HOS, view messages
- **Portal redirect:** Drivers are redirected to download the DriveCommand mobile app after accepting the invitation

Drivers can log into the web portal, but their primary experience is the mobile app.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I have multiple owners?**

Yes. You can add additional owners by inviting a team member and selecting the Owner role. Owners have full access and cannot have permissions restricted.

**Can a driver be promoted to dispatcher or manager?**

Yes. Edit the driver's team member record and change their role to Dispatcher or Manager. Adjust permissions as needed.

**What happens if I remove the only owner?**

You cannot remove the last owner. DriveCommand requires at least one owner per account for billing and administrative purposes. Add a new owner before removing the current one.

**Can team members reset their own passwords?**

Yes. Team members can use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page to reset their password via email.
