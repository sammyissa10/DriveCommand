# Revenue Report

<Callout variant="info">
Revenue Report provides detailed revenue analytics broken down by customer, lane, driver, and time period, helping you identify where your profits come from.
</Callout>

## What this is

A financial analytics tool for understanding revenue trends. See which customers generate the most revenue, which lanes are most profitable, which drivers haul the highest-value loads, and how revenue changes over time. Use these insights to focus on high-margin business and phase out unprofitable lanes.

<PlanBadge tier="business" /> This feature requires a Business plan or higher.

## How to use it

<StepFlow>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Access the report">
    Navigate to **Carrier → Reports → Revenue**. The default view shows total revenue for the current month.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Filter by date range">
    Use the date picker to select a custom range (last week, last month, last quarter, or custom dates).
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Group by dimension">
    Choose a grouping: by Customer, by Lane, by Driver, or by Time Period. The report recalculates to show revenue totals for each group.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="View trend charts">
    The chart section shows revenue trends over time. Use this to spot seasonal patterns or declining customer volume.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Export data">
    Click **Export CSV** to download the raw data for further analysis in Excel or your accounting software.
  </StepFlow.Step>
</StepFlow>

## Good to know

<Callout variant="tip" title="Pro Tip">
Run the Revenue by Customer report monthly to identify your top 5 customers. Focus retention efforts on these key accounts—losing one could significantly impact cash flow.
</Callout>

Revenue is calculated from invoices, not loads. If you've dispatched loads but haven't invoiced yet, they won't appear in revenue reports until the invoice is created.
