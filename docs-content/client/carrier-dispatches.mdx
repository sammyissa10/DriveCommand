# Dispatch Board

<Callout variant="info">
The Dispatch Board provides a centralized view of all ready-to-dispatch loads with real-time driver availability and truck status. Streamline your dispatch operations by quickly matching loads with available drivers and trucks.
</Callout>

## Overview

The Dispatch Board shows loads that are ready for assignment but not yet dispatched. Use it to:

- View all loads pending dispatch in one place
- Check driver availability and current status
- Match loads to trucks based on status and location
- Assign loads to routes with a single click

<PlanBadge tier="pro" /> This feature requires a Pro plan or higher.

## Create a dispatch

<StepFlow>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Open the Dispatch Board">
    Navigate to **Carrier Ops → Dispatches** in the sidebar.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Select a ready-to-dispatch load">
    Loads in PENDING status appear in the ready-to-dispatch list. Click on a load to view details.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Choose a driver">
    Review driver availability status:
    - **Available** — driver is ready for assignment
    - **On Route** — driver is currently assigned
    - **Off Duty** — driver is not available
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Select a truck">
    Truck status badges indicate readiness:
    - **Ready to Use** — all documents current, no maintenance alerts
    - **In Use** — truck assigned to another route
    - **In Maintenance** — truck unavailable
    - **Expired Docs** — requires document renewal before dispatch
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Dispatch the load">
    Click **Dispatch** to create the route and notify the driver. The load status automatically advances to DISPATCHED.
  </StepFlow.Step>
</StepFlow>

## Filtering and search

Use filters to narrow the dispatch board view:

- **By customer** — show loads from specific shippers or brokers
- **By pickup date** — prioritize loads by scheduled pickup time
- **By destination** — group loads going to similar areas for route optimization

<Callout variant="tip" title="Pro Tip">
Sort loads by pickup date to ensure time-sensitive shipments are dispatched first. Loads with pickup dates in the past are highlighted in red.
</Callout>

## Driver availability indicators

Driver cards show real-time status:

- **Green dot** — Available for dispatch
- **Yellow dot** — On route but approaching completion
- **Red dot** — Unavailable (on route, off duty, or HOS limit reached)

Click a driver card to view:
- Current route and load assignments
- Hours of Service remaining
- Last GPS location (if enabled)
- Compliance document status

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I dispatch multiple loads to one driver?**

Yes. If loads share similar pickup/delivery locations, you can assign them to the same route by adding multiple loads after the initial dispatch.

**What happens if I dispatch a load to a driver with expiring HOS?**

The system will show a warning if the driver's remaining HOS hours are insufficient for the estimated route duration. You can override the warning or choose a different driver.

**How do I un-dispatch a load?**

From the load detail page, click **Actions → Revert Status** to move the load back to PENDING. This removes the route assignment and frees up the driver.
