# Driver My Load

<Callout variant="info">
My Load is where you manage your current assignment. Update your status as you progress through pickup, transit, and delivery. Dispatch sees your updates in real-time.
</Callout>

## Overview

The My Load screen shows:

- **Load details** — BOL number, PO number, pickup/delivery addresses, scheduled times
- **Stop timeline** — Visual progress bar with pickup → delivery stops
- **Truck assignment** — Vehicle you're driving for this load
- **Contact info** — Shipper and receiver phone numbers (tap to call)
- **Status update button** — Advance to the next status

<PlanBadge tier="pro" /> This feature requires a Pro plan or higher.

## Load status workflow

Loads follow a forward-only progression. You cannot skip statuses or move backwards.

### Status rules

- **PENDING → PICKED_UP** — Confirms you've loaded the freight and obtained BOL signature
- **PICKED_UP → IN_TRANSIT** — Marks departure from shipper location
- **IN_TRANSIT → DELIVERED** — Confirms delivery completed and POD obtained

<Callout variant="warning" title="Cannot undo status changes">
Once you advance a load status, you cannot revert it. If you accidentally tap the wrong status, contact dispatch immediately to correct it.
</Callout>

## Update load status

<StepFlow>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Open your load">
    From the **Dashboard**, tap the active load card. Or navigate to **Loads → Active** tab.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Tap Update Status">
    The blue **Update Status** button shows the next available status (e.g., "Mark as Picked Up").
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Confirm the change">
    A confirmation modal appears showing:
    - Current status → New status
    - Timestamp of the change
    - Warning if you're advancing before the scheduled time
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Submit">
    Tap **Confirm**. The status updates immediately and dispatch receives a push notification.
  </StepFlow.Step>
</StepFlow>

### Offline status updates

If you lose cellular signal, status updates queue locally and sync when you reconnect. You'll see a blue "Syncing..." banner at the top of the screen. Queued updates flush automatically within 60 seconds of reconnection.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Pro Tip">
Update your status as soon as you complete each step. Timely updates help dispatch provide accurate ETAs to customers and prevent "where's my load?" calls.
</Callout>

## Stop timeline

The stop timeline shows:

- **Pickup stop** — Address, scheduled time, contact name/phone
- **Delivery stop** — Address, scheduled time, contact name/phone
- **Completed stops** — Green checkmark indicator
- **Current stop** — Blue dot indicator
- **Upcoming stops** — Gray dot indicator

For multi-stop routes (more than one pickup or delivery), the timeline shows all stops in sequence order.

## Load details

### Freight information

- **BOL Number** — Bill of Lading reference
- **PO Number** — Customer purchase order
- **Commodity** — Type of freight (e.g., "Refrigerated Food", "Building Materials")
- **Weight** — Total load weight in pounds
- **Pieces** — Number of pallets or units

### Schedule

- **Pickup scheduled** — When you need to arrive at shipper
- **Delivery scheduled** — When freight is due at receiver
- **Created date** — When dispatch created the load

### Contact info

Tap phone numbers to call directly:

- **Pickup contact** — Shipper dock supervisor
- **Delivery contact** — Receiver dock supervisor
- **Dispatch** — Your fleet manager

## Load-scoped messages

At the bottom of the load screen, you'll see a **Messages** button. This opens a thread for this specific load where you can:

- Ask dispatch questions about the load
- Report delays or issues
- Confirm special instructions

Messages are scoped to this load only. General fleet messages go through the main **Messages** tab.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I see loads I've already delivered?**

Yes. Go to **Loads → History** tab to see all completed loads. Tap any load to view final details and delivery timestamp.

**What if I need to cancel a load?**

Drivers cannot cancel loads. Contact dispatch via the Messages tab or phone. Only dispatchers can cancel or reassign loads.

**Why can't I update my status?**

Common reasons:
- You're offline (updates will queue and sync when reconnected)
- The load is already at DELIVERED status
- Dispatch has locked the load for editing (rare)

If none of these apply, contact support.
