# Driver Dashboard

<Callout variant="info">
The Driver Dashboard is your mobile command center. See your active load, today's stats, important alerts, and quick access to your route — all in one screen optimized for on-the-go use.
</Callout>

## Overview

When you open the DriveCommand mobile app, the dashboard is the first thing you see. It's designed to answer three key questions:

1. **What am I hauling today?** — Active load card with pickup/delivery details
2. **How am I doing?** — Stat chips showing deliveries, miles, and HOS hours
3. **What needs my attention?** — Alert banners for upcoming deadlines or issues

<PlanBadge tier="pro" /> This feature requires a Pro plan or higher.

## Dashboard layout

### Active load card

The top section shows your currently assigned load (if you have one):

- **Load ID** — Reference number for dispatch communication
- **Pickup location** — City and state of origin
- **Delivery location** — Final destination
- **Scheduled pickup time** — When you need to be at the shipper
- **Status** — PENDING, PICKED_UP, IN_TRANSIT, or DELIVERED

Tap the load card to view full load details including stop timeline, BOL number, and contact info.

<Callout variant="tip" title="No active load?">
If you don't have an active load, the card shows "No Active Load" with a message to contact dispatch. The card disappears once you complete your delivery.
</Callout>

### Stat chips

Three compact stat cards show your performance for today:

- **Deliveries** — Number of loads delivered today
- **Miles** — Total miles driven in current duty period
- **HOS Remaining** — Hours left in your 14-hour window

These update automatically as you advance load statuses and log HOS changes.

### My Route shortcut

If you're assigned to a multi-stop route, a "My Route" button appears below the load card. Tap it to see:

- Full route timeline with all stops
- Truck assignment details
- Route-scoped messages from dispatch

<FeatureCard slug="driver-my-route" />

### Alerts section

Important notifications appear as colored banners:

- **Red alerts** — HOS violation warnings, document expirations
- **Yellow alerts** — Upcoming deadlines (HOS approaching limit, maintenance due)
- **Blue alerts** — New messages from dispatch, route updates

Tap any alert to jump directly to the relevant screen (Hours, Documents, Messages, etc.).

## Quick actions

From the dashboard, use the bottom navigation to access:

- **Loads** — View load history and current assignment details
- **Hours** — Log HOS status changes
- **Incidents** — Report accidents or vehicle issues
- **Documents** — Upload compliance docs (license, medical card)
- **Messages** — Communicate with dispatch
- **More** — Settings, support, and additional tools

<Callout variant="success">
The dashboard refreshes automatically when you return to the app from the background. No need to manually pull-to-refresh.
</Callout>

## Mobile-first design

The driver dashboard is optimized for one-handed use while stationary:

- Large touch targets (minimum 48px)
- High contrast text readable in sunlight
- Auto dark mode during nighttime hours
- Minimal scrolling — key info above the fold

## Frequently asked questions

**Why don't I see my load on the dashboard?**

Loads appear on the dashboard only after dispatch assigns them to you. If you've been notified about a load but don't see it, check your push notifications or contact dispatch.

**Can I change my HOS status from the dashboard?**

No. Tap the **Hours** tab in the bottom navigation to log duty status changes. The dashboard only displays your remaining hours.

**What does the GPS dot in the tab bar mean?**

Green = GPS tracking active, Yellow = GPS paused, Red = GPS disabled. Background GPS tracking is required for automatic geofencing alerts and route progress updates.
