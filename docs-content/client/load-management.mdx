# Managing Your Loads

<Callout variant="info">
Loads are the heart of your dispatch operation. Each load represents a shipment from origin to destination, containing all the details you need to move freight efficiently.
</Callout>

## What is a Load?

A load in DriveCommand represents a single shipment from a pickup location to a delivery destination. Each load includes customer information, origin and destination addresses, pickup and delivery dates, rate information, and optional details like commodity type, weight, and special handling instructions.

Loads can exist independently or be linked to multi-stop routes for complex trips. When you assign a load to a route, DriveCommand automatically creates the corresponding route stops and keeps everything in sync.

## Creating a New Load

<StepFlow>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Navigate to Loads">
    From the sidebar, click **Loads** to open the load management screen. You'll see a list of all your loads organized by status (Pending, Dispatched, In Transit, Delivered).
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Click Create Load">
    Click the **+ New Load** button in the top right corner to open the load creation form.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Enter Load Details">
    Fill in the required fields: customer name, origin address, destination address, pickup date, and rate. You can also add optional information like delivery date, weight, commodity type, and internal notes. DriveCommand will automatically calculate the distance using real road miles.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Dispatch or Save">
    Click **Save as Pending** to save the load for later assignment, or **Dispatch Now** to immediately assign a driver and truck. If you choose to dispatch, you'll select the driver and vehicle in the next step.
  </StepFlow.Step>
</StepFlow>

## Dispatch Methods Comparison

DriveCommand offers multiple ways to assign loads to drivers. Choose the method that fits your workflow.

<ComparisonTable
  headers={["Method", "Best For", "Time to Dispatch"]}

Manual dispatch gives you full control for custom situations. Route assignment is perfect when you're building complex multi-stop trips. AI suggestions help you make the best choice quickly when you have multiple available drivers.
