# Invoicing

<Callout variant="info">
DriveCommand invoicing is built for trucking companies with industry-standard freight fields, fuel surcharge auto-calculation, and typed line items. Generate professional PDF invoices for customers and track payment status.
</Callout>

## Overview

Use invoicing to:

- **Create professional invoices** — trucking-standard layout with BOL, PRO, PO numbers, commodity details, and weight
- **Calculate fuel surcharges** — FSC auto-calculates as a percentage of linehaul charges
- **Track payments** — mark invoices as sent, paid, or overdue with payment date tracking
- **Generate PDFs** — download or email professional PDF invoices to customers

<PlanBadge tier="pro" /> This feature requires a Pro plan or higher.

## Create an invoice

<StepFlow>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Open Invoices">
    Navigate to **Finance → Invoices** in the sidebar.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Click New Invoice">
    The invoice creation form opens.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Select customer and load">
    Choose the customer from your CRM and optionally link a load. Load details auto-populate freight fields.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Enter freight details">
    Expand the **Freight Details** section and enter:
    - **BOL Number** — Bill of Lading number
    - **PRO Number** — Progressive number
    - **PO Number** — Purchase Order number
    - **Commodity** — cargo type (e.g., "Electronics", "Produce")
    - **Weight** — total weight in pounds
    - **Pieces** — number of pieces or pallets
    - **Loaded Miles** — actual miles driven with cargo
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Add line items">
    Add invoice line items using quick-add buttons or manually:
    - **Linehaul** — base freight charge (per mile or flat rate)
    - **Fuel Surcharge (FSC)** — auto-calculates as percentage of linehaul
    - **Detention** — hourly waiting charges
    - **Stop-Off** — additional stop fees
    - **Lumper** — loading/unloading fees
    - **Accessorial** — other accessorial charges
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Review and save">
    Verify totals, set invoice date and due date, then click **Save Invoice**.
  </StepFlow.Step>
</StepFlow>

## Fuel surcharge (FSC) calculation

Fuel surcharge auto-calculates based on linehaul charges:

1. Add a **Linehaul** line item with total amount
2. Click **Add FSC** quick-add button
3. Enter FSC percentage (e.g., 25%)
4. FSC amount auto-calculates: `linehaul total × (FSC% / 100)`
5. Helper text shows calculation (e.g., "25% of $1,200.00 linehaul = $300.00")

The FSC line item updates automatically if you change the linehaul amount.

## Generate PDF invoice

Click **Download PDF** on any invoice detail page to generate a professional trucking invoice with:

- Company logo and billing address
- Customer name and address
- Invoice number, date, and due date
- Freight details (BOL/PRO/PO, commodity, weight, pieces, miles)
- Itemized line items with quantities, rates, and totals
- Subtotal, tax (if applicable), and total amount due
- Payment terms and instructions

Email the PDF directly to customers or save for your records.

## Payment tracking

Track invoice payments:

1. Open the invoice detail page
2. Click **Mark as Paid**
3. Enter the payment date
4. Optionally add payment notes (check number, wire transfer reference)
5. Invoice status updates to **Paid**

The invoice list shows payment status with color-coded badges:
- **Draft** — gray
- **Sent** — amber
- **Paid** — green
- **Overdue** — red (sent invoices past due date)

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I edit an invoice after marking it as paid?**

Yes. You can edit any invoice field at any time. If you edit a paid invoice, the payment status remains "Paid" unless you explicitly change it.

**Does DriveCommand support factoring integrations?**

Not yet. Factoring integration is planned for a future release. For now, export invoice PDFs and upload to your factoring portal.

**Can I customize the invoice PDF template?**

Not yet. Custom invoice templates with your logo and branding are planned for the Business plan.
