# Driver Workflow Tasks

<Callout variant="info">
Workflow Tasks help you complete standardized procedures step-by-step. From pre-trip inspections to load securement checklists, tasks ensure nothing gets missed and provide proof of completion for compliance.
</Callout>

## Overview

Your fleet manager can assign task checklists that must be completed at specific times:

- **Pre-trip inspections** — Required before every shift
- **Load securement checklists** — Verify cargo is properly secured
- **Delivery confirmation** — Capture POD signatures and photos
- **Post-trip inspections** — End-of-shift vehicle checks
- **DVIR (Driver Vehicle Inspection Report)** — DOT-required daily inspections

Each checklist contains multiple tasks. You complete them one by one, marking each as done when finished.

<PlanBadge tier="pro" /> This feature requires a Pro plan or higher.

## Task types

Tasks can require different types of completion:

### Simple checkbox tasks

Tap to mark complete. Examples:

- Check tire pressure
- Verify lights are working
- Confirm load straps are tight

### Photo tasks

Tap to capture a photo with your camera. The photo is uploaded as proof. Examples:

- Photo of secured cargo
- Damage documentation
- Odometer reading

### Signature tasks

Tap to open signature pad. Sign with your finger or stylus. Examples:

- DVIR sign-off
- Customer delivery receipt
- Inspection acknowledgment

### Text entry tasks

Tap to type a response. Examples:

- Odometer reading (numeric input)
- Notes about vehicle condition
- Customer name for POD

<Callout variant="tip" title="Why task types matter">
Task types ensure you provide the right kind of proof. A photo task won't let you proceed without capturing an image. This prevents accidental skips and maintains compliance records.
</Callout>

## Complete a task checklist

<StepFlow>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Open Tasks">
    Navigate to **More → Tasks** (or tap a task notification if you received one).
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Select active checklist">
    Tap the checklist you need to complete. Active checklists are at the top with a "In Progress" badge.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Read step instructions">
    Each task shows instructions at the top (e.g., "Check all tires for proper inflation and visible damage").
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Complete the task">
    Depending on task type:
    - **Checkbox** — Tap the checkbox to mark done
    - **Photo** — Tap "Capture Photo", take the picture, confirm
    - **Signature** — Tap "Sign Here", draw signature, tap "Done"
    - **Text** — Tap the input field, type the required info, tap "Next"
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Move to next task">
    The next incomplete task automatically appears. Repeat until all tasks are done.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Submit checklist">
    After completing all tasks, tap **Submit Checklist**. You'll see a confirmation that the checklist was submitted successfully.
  </StepFlow.Step>
</StepFlow>

<Callout variant="warning" title="Cannot skip tasks">
Checklists enforce sequential completion. You cannot skip a task or submit a checklist with incomplete tasks. If a task doesn't apply, check with your fleet manager — they may need to remove it from the template.
</Callout>

## Progress indicator

At the top of each checklist, you'll see:

- **Progress bar** — Visual indicator (e.g., "3 of 12 tasks complete")
- **Percentage** — e.g., "25% complete"
- **Estimated time remaining** — Based on average completion time

This helps you gauge how much longer the checklist will take.

## Auto-start checklists

Some checklists start automatically based on triggers:

- **Pre-trip checklist** — Auto-starts when you log your first HOS status change of the day
- **Load securement** — Auto-starts when you advance a load to PICKED_UP status
- **Post-trip checklist** — Auto-starts when you go Off Duty at end of shift

You'll receive a push notification when an auto-start checklist is created.

## View completed checklists

Tap **Tasks → History** to see all completed checklists. Each entry shows:

- Checklist name
- Completion date and time
- All task responses (photos, signatures, text entries)

Completed checklists are stored for compliance audits and can be exported by your fleet manager.

## Checklist templates

Your fleet manager creates checklist templates. You cannot create or edit templates from the driver app.

If a checklist doesn't match your workflow or includes irrelevant tasks, contact your fleet manager to update the template.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I save a checklist and finish it later?**

Yes. Checklists auto-save as you complete tasks. You can close the app and resume later. However, some checklists (like pre-trip) may be time-sensitive — check with your fleet manager about deadlines.

**What if I can't complete a task?**

Contact your fleet manager immediately. Example: "Task says check trailer lights, but I'm driving a bobtail today." They can remove the task or mark the checklist as not applicable.

**Do checklists work offline?**

Yes. You can complete checklists offline. Photos and signatures save locally and upload when you reconnect. Completed checklists sync automatically.

**Can I edit a task after marking it complete?**

No. Once a task is marked complete, it cannot be edited. This maintains audit integrity. If you made an error, contact your fleet manager — they can append notes or request a new checklist.
