# Safety Analytics

<Callout variant="info">
Safety Analytics aggregates driver safety data from GPS tracking, incident reports, and ELD integrations to calculate safety scores and identify high-risk drivers. Use this data to reduce insurance costs and improve fleet safety.
</Callout>

## Overview

The Safety Analytics dashboard provides fleet-wide safety metrics:

- **Fleet safety score** — average across all drivers (0-100)
- **Harsh braking events** — sudden deceleration incidents
- **Speeding violations** — tracked via GPS and ELD
- **Incident reports** — driver-reported accidents and near-misses
- **Driver rankings** — sorted by safety score
- **Trend charts** — weekly and monthly safety trends

<PlanBadge tier="business" /> This feature requires a Business plan or higher.

## Understand safety scores

Driver safety scores range from 0 to 100 and are calculated from multiple data sources:

Score categories:

- **90-100**: Excellent (green badge)
- **70-89**: Good (yellow badge)
- **Below 70**: Needs improvement (red badge)

Scores update in real-time as new events are logged.

## Track harsh braking events

Harsh braking events are detected when a driver decelerates faster than a safe threshold (typically 8+ mph per second):

- **GPS-based detection** — calculated from GPS speed changes
- **ELD integration** — imported from Samsara, Motive/KeepTruckin, or other connected ELDs
- **Event details** — timestamp, location, speed before/after

View harsh braking events on the Safety Analytics dashboard or on individual driver detail pages.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Pro Tip">
Harsh braking often indicates following too closely or distracted driving. Use these events to coach drivers on maintaining safe following distances.
</Callout>

## Monitor speeding violations

Speeding events are logged when drivers exceed speed limits:

- **10+ mph over limit**: Yellow warning
- **20+ mph over limit**: Red violation

Speeding data comes from:

- **GPS tracking** — compared against posted speed limit databases
- **ELD integration** — imported from connected devices

Frequent speeding violations lower a driver's safety score and increase insurance risk.

## Review incident reports

Drivers can report incidents from the Driver Portal:

- **Accident** — collision with another vehicle or object
- **Near-miss** — close call that could have been an accident
- **Injury** — driver or passenger injury
- **Property damage** — damage to cargo or equipment

Each incident includes:

- **Severity** — Low, Medium, High, Critical
- **Category** — Accident, Near-Miss, Injury, Property Damage
- **Description** — driver's narrative
- **Photos** — evidence captured at the scene
- **Location and timestamp**

Incidents with High or Critical severity trigger immediate alerts to fleet managers.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Compliance Requirement">
All accidents involving injury, fatality, or tow-away must be reported to FMCSA within 10 days. Use incident reports to ensure timely compliance.
</Callout>

## Identify high-risk drivers

The driver ranking table shows all drivers sorted by safety score:

1. Navigate to **Safety Analytics**
2. Scroll to **Driver Rankings** section
3. Click on any driver to view detailed safety history

Drivers with red badges (score below 70) should receive immediate coaching or retraining.

## View safety trends

Safety trend charts show fleet-wide metrics over time:

- **Weekly trends** — last 8 weeks
- **Monthly trends** — last 12 months
- **Event type breakdown** — harsh braking vs. speeding vs. incidents

Use trend data to measure the effectiveness of safety programs and training initiatives.

<Callout variant="success">
If you see safety scores improving over time, share the results with your team. Positive reinforcement encourages continued safe driving.
</Callout>

## Export safety reports

Generate safety reports for insurance audits or compliance reviews:

1. Navigate to **Safety Analytics**
2. Click **Export Report**
3. Select date range
4. Choose format (PDF or CSV)
5. Click **Download**

Reports include fleet summary, driver-level details, and event logs.

## Frequently asked questions

**How often do safety scores update?**

Safety scores recalculate in real-time whenever a new event is logged (harsh braking, speeding, incident report).

**Can I adjust the harsh braking threshold?**

Not currently. The threshold is set at 8 mph/second based on industry standards. Custom thresholds are planned for a future release.

**What if a driver disputes a speeding violation?**

Review the GPS event details including timestamp, location, and speed. Compare against posted speed limits and road conditions. Contact support if data appears incorrect.

**Do safety scores affect driver pay?**

Only if you configure safety-based bonuses in your payroll structure. DriveCommand does not automatically adjust driver pay based on safety scores.
