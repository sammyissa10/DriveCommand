# IFTA Reporting

<Callout variant="info">
IFTA (International Fuel Tax Agreement) reporting is automated with DriveCommand. GPS data calculates state-by-state mileage, fuel logs allocate fuel purchases by jurisdiction, and quarterly reports are generated with one click.
</Callout>

## Overview

IFTA reporting in DriveCommand:

- **GPS-based mileage allocation** — automatically calculate miles driven in each state using GPS tracking data
- **Fuel purchase tracking** — log fuel fill-ups by state to allocate gallons purchased
- **Quarterly reports** — generate IFTA-compliant reports for submission to your base jurisdiction
- **Export to PDF or CSV** — download reports for filing or accountant review
- **Multi-truck support** — separate reports for each truck or combined fleet report

<PlanBadge tier="business" /> This feature requires a Business plan or higher.

## How IFTA reporting works

IFTA requires trucking companies to report:

1. **Total miles driven per state** — how many miles your trucks drove in each jurisdiction
2. **Total fuel purchased per state** — how many gallons you bought in each jurisdiction
3. **Tax-paid gallons** — gallons purchased with fuel tax already included (most states)
4. **Net tax owed or credit** — the difference between fuel tax owed (based on miles) and fuel tax paid (based on purchases)

DriveCommand automates steps 1 and 2 using GPS tracking and fuel logs.

## GPS-based mileage allocation

DriveCommand uses GPS reports from your trucks to calculate mileage per state:

1. GPS pings are recorded every 30 seconds while trucks are moving (via mobile app or ELD integration)
2. The system uses reverse geocoding to determine which state each GPS point is in
3. Distance between consecutive GPS points is calculated
4. Mileage is summed per state for the quarter

**Accuracy:** GPS-based mileage is typically accurate within 1-2% of actual odometer miles. The system filters out stationary GPS pings (e.g., parked trucks) to avoid false mileage.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Important">
GPS tracking must be enabled for accurate IFTA reporting. Ensure drivers have the mobile app installed and GPS permissions enabled, or connect an ELD integration.
</Callout>

## Fuel purchase tracking

Log fuel fill-ups with purchase location (city and state):

When you log a fuel fill-up, DriveCommand:

1. Records the gallons purchased
2. Records the state where fuel was purchased (from the location field)
3. Allocates fuel taxes paid based on that state's tax rate
4. Includes the entry in the quarterly IFTA report

**Important:** Always enter the correct state for each fuel fill-up. Incorrect locations will cause inaccurate tax allocation.

## IFTA report structure

The generated report includes:

**Header:**
- Company name, IFTA account number, and base jurisdiction
- Quarter and year
- Truck details (VIN, license plate, unit number)

**State-by-state breakdown:**
- State abbreviation (e.g., CA, TX, OH)
- Total miles driven in state
- Total gallons purchased in state
- Fuel tax rate for state (from IFTA tax tables)
- Tax-paid gallons
- Net gallons (miles-based consumption minus purchased gallons)
- Tax owed or credit

**Summary:**
- Total miles driven (all states)
- Total gallons purchased (all states)
- Net tax owed or credit (sum across all states)

## Filing the report

DriveCommand generates IFTA-compliant reports but does not file them directly with your base jurisdiction. To file:

1. Download the PDF or CSV report from DriveCommand
2. Review for accuracy (verify mileage and fuel totals match your records)
3. Submit to your base jurisdiction via their filing portal (most states support electronic filing)
4. Pay any net tax owed or claim credit

**Filing deadlines:** IFTA reports are due by the last day of the month following the end of the quarter:
- Q1 (Jan-Mar) — due April 30
- Q2 (Apr-Jun) — due July 31
- Q3 (Jul-Sep) — due October 31
- Q4 (Oct-Dec) — due January 31

## Troubleshooting

**Issue: Missing mileage for a state**

**Cause:** GPS tracking was disabled or the driver's phone lost signal.

**Fix:** Manually add mileage from driver logs or ELD reports. Edit the IFTA report before exporting.

**Issue: Fuel purchases not appearing**

**Cause:** Fuel logs were not entered or the purchase state was entered incorrectly.

**Fix:** Go to Fuel Dashboard and verify all fill-ups for the quarter are logged with correct states.

**Issue: Total miles don't match odometer**

**Cause:** GPS-based mileage calculation includes deadhead and loaded miles. Odometer may include personal use or off-duty driving.

**Fix:** Filter GPS data by on-duty time only (requires HOS integration). Adjust mileage manually if needed.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need GPS tracking for IFTA reporting?**

Yes. IFTA requires state-by-state mileage, which is calculated from GPS data. Without GPS tracking, you'll need to manually enter mileage per state.

**Can I edit mileage or fuel data after generating a report?**

Yes. Edit fuel logs or manually adjust mileage in the IFTA report before exporting. Changes are saved to the report draft but do not affect historical GPS or fuel data.

**Does DriveCommand calculate tax rates for each state?**

Yes. DriveCommand uses the latest IFTA tax rate tables (updated quarterly) to calculate tax owed or credit per state. Rates are built into the system.
