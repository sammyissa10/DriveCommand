# Contract Management

<Callout variant="info">
Contract Management helps you create, track, and renew carrier contracts with shippers, storing rate tables and terms in one central location.
</Callout>

## What this is

A system for managing formal agreements with your customers. Store contract terms, effective dates, expiration dates, and lane-specific rate tables. Set reminders for upcoming renewals so you never lose negotiated rates due to an expired contract.

<PlanBadge tier="business" /> This feature requires a Business plan or higher.

## How to use it

<StepFlow>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Create a contract">
    Navigate to **Carrier → Contracts** and click **New Contract**. Select the client from your existing customer list.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Set contract dates">
    Enter the effective start date and expiration date. DriveCommand will automatically flag contracts expiring within 30 days.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Add rate tables">
    Define rates per lane (origin-destination pairs) or per mile. You can upload a CSV rate sheet or enter rates manually in the table editor.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Attach contract document">
    Upload the signed PDF contract for reference. This is stored securely and accessible to anyone with Contract Management permissions.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Monitor renewal dates">
    The Contracts dashboard shows all contracts expiring in the next 60 days. Click **Renew** to duplicate the contract with a new effective period.
  </StepFlow.Step>
</StepFlow>

## Good to know

<Callout variant="tip" title="Pro Tip">
Set up rate tables in your contracts, then reference them when creating loads. DriveCommand can auto-populate the load rate based on the contract terms, reducing data entry errors.
</Callout>

Contracts can be linked to individual loads for audit trails. When a contract expires, loads created under that contract remain tied to it for historical reporting.
