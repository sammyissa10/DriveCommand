# Performance Report

<Callout variant="info">
Performance Report tracks key operational metrics like on-time delivery rate, truck utilization, cost-per-mile, and driver efficiency to help you optimize fleet performance.
</Callout>

## What this is

An operational dashboard for measuring fleet efficiency. See what percentage of loads are delivered on time, how many miles each truck drives per month, your average cost per mile (including fuel, maintenance, driver pay), and which drivers are most productive. Use these metrics to identify underutilized assets and operational bottlenecks.

<PlanBadge tier="business" /> This feature requires a Business plan or higher.

## How to use it

<StepFlow>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Open the performance report">
    Go to **Carrier → Reports → Performance**. Select a date range (default is the last 30 days).
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Review on-time delivery rate">
    The top metric shows what percentage of loads were delivered by the promised date. Industry standard is 95%+. If you're below that, investigate delays.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Check truck utilization">
    Utilization shows miles driven vs. miles available (total days × average daily miles). Low utilization means trucks are sitting idle—look for more freight.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Analyze cost-per-mile">
    This metric combines fuel, maintenance, insurance, and driver pay divided by total miles. Compare to industry benchmarks ($1.50-$2.00 per mile for dry van).
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Review driver efficiency">
    See which drivers complete the most loads or drive the most miles per month. Top performers can mentor newer drivers to improve fleet-wide efficiency.
  </StepFlow.Step>
</StepFlow>

## Good to know

<Callout variant="tip" title="Pro Tip">
Track cost-per-mile monthly. If it's increasing, drill into fuel and maintenance costs to identify the cause. Rising CPM without rising rates erodes your profit margins.
</Callout>

On-time delivery is calculated from the load's scheduled delivery date vs. actual delivery timestamp. Ensure drivers mark loads as delivered promptly for accurate metrics.
