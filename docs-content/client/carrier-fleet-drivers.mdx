# Carrier Driver Management

<Callout variant="info">
Carrier Driver Management gives you a complete view of your driver roster with real-time compliance status, current load assignments, and invitation tools for onboarding new drivers.
</Callout>

## What this is

Your driver roster command center. View all drivers with at-a-glance compliance indicators (green, amber, red dots for license/medical expiry), see who's currently on a load, and manage driver invitations. This is where you monitor driver availability and ensure everyone stays compliant before dispatch.

Carrier Driver Management is the owner-portal view of drivers; drivers themselves use the Driver Portal for their own tasks and documents.

## How to use it

<StepFlow>
  <StepFlow.Step title="View driver list">
    Go to **Carrier → Fleet → Drivers** to see all drivers. Each driver shows name, compliance dots, current load (if any), and last active timestamp.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Check compliance status">
    Green dot = all documents valid. Amber = expiring within 30 days. Red = expired or missing required docs. Click the dot to see which documents need attention.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Invite a new driver">
    Click **Invite Driver**, enter their email and phone, optionally assign permissions, and send the invitation. They'll receive an email with a sign-up link.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="View driver detail">
    Click any driver name to see their profile: current load, document status, recent HOS entries, incident reports, and contact info.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Manage driver assignments">
    From the driver detail page, use quick actions to assign them to a load, send a message, or review their load history.
  </StepFlow.Step>
</StepFlow>

## Good to know

<Callout variant="tip" title="Pro Tip">
Before dispatching a driver, always check their compliance dot. A red dot means they're not roadside-ready. Upload or renew the missing document before assigning them to a new load to avoid violations.
</Callout>

Compliance dots update in real-time when drivers upload documents from their mobile app. You don't need to manually refresh—the status changes as soon as a document is processed.
