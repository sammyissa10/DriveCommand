# AR Aging Report

<Callout variant="info">
The AR Aging Report shows which invoices are overdue and by how long, organized into 30/60/90 day buckets to help you collect receivables faster.
</Callout>

## What this is

A cash flow management tool. See all outstanding invoices grouped by how long they've been overdue: Current (not yet due), 1-30 days overdue, 31-60 days, 61-90 days, and 90+ days. Use this to prioritize collection calls and identify customers with chronic late payment issues.

<PlanBadge tier="business" /> This feature requires a Business plan or higher.

## How to use it

<StepFlow>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Access the report">
    This report has no menu entry on either desktop or mobile — open it directly at `/carrier/reports/aging`. The report loads with all unpaid invoices.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Review aging buckets">
    Each customer has columns showing invoice totals in each bucket: Current, 1-30 days, 31-60 days, 61-90 days, 90+ days.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Identify overdue accounts">
    Focus on the 31-60 and 61-90 day columns. These invoices are significantly overdue and should be prioritized for follow-up.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Click for invoice detail">
    Click any customer name to see the specific invoices in each bucket with invoice numbers, dates, and amounts.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Take collection action">
    Use the contact info from the customer record to call or email about overdue invoices. Mark invoices as disputed if the customer claims an issue.
  </StepFlow.Step>
</StepFlow>

## Good to know

<Callout variant="tip" title="Pro Tip">
Run this report weekly. Catching overdue invoices early increases your collection rate. A 90+ day overdue invoice is much harder to collect than a 15-day overdue one.
</Callout>

The aging calculation is based on invoice due date, not invoice creation date. If you set Net 30 terms, the clock starts 30 days after the invoice date.
