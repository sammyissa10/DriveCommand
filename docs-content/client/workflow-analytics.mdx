# Workflow Analytics

<Callout variant="info">
Workflow Analytics shows how long workflows take to complete, which steps are bottlenecks, and what percentage of workflows are finished vs. abandoned.
</Callout>

## What this is

A performance measurement tool for your workflows. See completion rates (what % of started checklists are actually finished), average duration per workflow, which steps take the longest, and which steps are most often skipped.

<PlanBadge tier="business" /> This feature requires a Business plan or higher.

## How to use it

<StepFlow>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Access workflow analytics">
    Navigate to **Workflows → Analytics**. The dashboard loads with completion metrics for the last 30 days.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Review completion rate">
    The top card shows completion rate: percentage of workflows that were finished. Industry best practice is 90%+. If you're below that, investigate why workflows are abandoned.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Identify bottlenecks">
    The bottleneck chart shows which steps have the longest average completion time. If "Upload rate confirmation" takes 10+ minutes, consider simplifying that step.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Check skip rate">
    The skip rate table shows which optional steps are skipped most often. If "Verify trailer seal" is skipped 80% of the time, either make it required or remove it.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Drill into specific workflows">
    Click any workflow name to see its individual analytics: completion trend over time, average duration, and step-by-step timing breakdown.
  </StepFlow.Step>
</StepFlow>

## Good to know

<Callout variant="tip" title="Pro Tip">
Run workflow analytics monthly. Use the data to refine your checklists—remove unnecessary steps, clarify confusing instructions, and make frequently skipped steps optional.
</Callout>

Analytics are calculated from all workflow instances, not just completed ones. Abandoned workflows count toward the completion rate to give you a realistic view of adoption.
