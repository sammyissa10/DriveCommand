# Legacy Driver Management

<Callout variant="info">
Legacy Driver Management provides driver profile management, email-based invitation flow, document tracking, and safety scoring. For the enhanced interface with real-time status and advanced features, see Carrier Driver Management.
</Callout>

## Overview

The driver list shows all active and invited drivers with key information:

- **Name and contact information**
- **Email and phone number**
- **License number and CDL class**
- **Compliance status** — green (current), yellow (expiring soon), red (expired)
- **Current load assignment**
- **Safety score** — calculated from incident reports and safety events

<PlanBadge tier="pro" /> This feature requires a Pro plan or higher.

## Invite a driver

DriveCommand uses email invitations to onboard drivers securely:

<StepFlow>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Navigate to Drivers">
    In the sidebar, click **Drivers** under Legacy Features.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Click Invite Driver">
    The invitation form appears.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Enter driver details">
    Required fields:
    - **First and last name**
    - **Email address** — driver will receive invitation here
    - **Phone number**
    - **License number and state**
    - **CDL class** (A, B, or C)
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Set permissions (optional)">
    If using Team Permissions, configure driver access levels before sending the invite.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Send invitation">
    Click **Send Invitation**. The driver receives an email with a secure acceptance link.
  </StepFlow.Step>
</StepFlow>

<ProcessDiagram steps={[
  { label: "Invite Sent", description: "Driver receives email with unique token link" },
  { label: "Driver Accepts", description: "Driver creates password and verifies email" },
  { label: "Profile Complete", description: "Driver appears as Active and can access Driver Portal" }
]} />

<Callout variant="tip" title="Pro Tip">
Drivers must accept the invitation within 7 days. After 7 days, the invitation link expires and you'll need to resend it.
</Callout>

## Track driver documents

Each driver must maintain three core compliance documents:

1. **Commercial Driver's License (CDL)** — valid CDL with proper class
2. **Medical Certificate** — DOT medical examiner's certificate
3. **MVR (Motor Vehicle Record)** — annual driving record check

To upload driver documents:

1. Click the driver in the list
2. Scroll to **Documents** section
3. Click **Upload Document**
4. Select document type, expiry date, and file
5. Click **Upload**

Documents expiring within 30 days show a yellow warning. Expired documents show a red alert.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Compliance Requirement">
Drivers with expired CDL or medical certificates cannot be assigned to loads. Ensure all documents are current before dispatch.
</Callout>

## Monitor safety scores

Driver safety scores are calculated from multiple data sources:

- **Incident reports** — accidents, near-misses, and safety violations
- **Harsh braking events** — from GPS tracking or ELD integration
- **Speeding violations** — tracked via GPS or ELD
- **HOS violations** — hours of service rule breaks

Scores range from 0 to 100:

- **90-100**: Excellent (green)
- **70-89**: Good (yellow)
- **Below 70**: Needs improvement (red)

View detailed safety analytics and trends on the Safety Analytics page.

## View driver activity

The driver detail page shows:

- **Current load** — active shipment in progress
- **Load history** — past 90 days of completed loads
- **Total miles driven** — lifetime and last 30 days
- **Incident history** — logged incidents with severity and dates
- **Document status** — compliance overview with expiry dates

Use this information to evaluate driver performance and identify training opportunities.

## Deactivate a driver

If a driver leaves your company:

1. Open the driver detail page
2. Click **Actions → Deactivate Driver**
3. Confirm deactivation

Deactivated drivers cannot log in to the Driver Portal and won't appear in dispatch assignment lists. Their historical data remains in the system for reporting.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Important">
Deactivated drivers cannot be reactivated. If a driver returns, you must send a new invitation.
</Callout>

## Frequently asked questions

**What's the difference between Drivers and Carrier Drivers?**

Drivers is the original legacy interface. Carrier Drivers is the newer interface with real-time status updates, compliance dots, and current load visibility. Both manage the same driver data.

**Can a driver update their own documents?**

Yes. Drivers can upload documents via the Driver Portal under Documents. You'll receive a notification when they upload new files.

**How do I resend an invitation?**

Open the driver detail page and click **Resend Invitation**. The previous invitation link is invalidated and a new one is sent.

**What happens to driver data when they're deactivated?**

All historical data (loads, incidents, safety scores) is preserved for reporting. Only login access and dispatch availability are revoked.
