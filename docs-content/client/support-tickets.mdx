# Support Tickets

<Callout variant="info">
Need help? Create a support ticket directly from the app and our team will respond within 24 hours.
</Callout>

## Creating a Support Ticket

Support tickets are available in both the Owner and Driver portals. Look for the floating support button in the bottom-right corner of any page.

<StepFlow>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Open the Support Form">
    Click the "Help" button in the bottom-right corner of any page. The support form will slide up from the bottom.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Fill in Details">
    Enter a descriptive subject line and explain your issue in the description field. The more details you provide, the faster we can help.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Add Screenshots or Files">
    Click "Add Screenshot" to automatically capture the current page, or click "Attach File" to upload a document, photo, or log file.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Submit">
    Click "Submit Ticket" and you'll receive a confirmation email. Our support team will reply within 24 hours.
  </StepFlow.Step>
</StepFlow>

## Screenshot Auto-Capture

The support form includes a powerful screenshot feature that automatically captures your current screen when you click "Add Screenshot". This helps our team see exactly what you're looking at.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Pro Tip">
If you're experiencing a visual bug or confusing UI element, always add a screenshot. It helps us diagnose and fix issues much faster.
</Callout>

## Tracking Your Tickets

All your support tickets are visible in the Help Center:

1. Navigate to **Help** in the main menu
2. Click **My Tickets**
3. See all open and resolved tickets with status updates

You'll receive email notifications when our team replies to your ticket.

## What to Include

For the fastest resolution, include:

- **Clear subject line** — "Cannot update load status" is better than "Help needed"
- **Steps to reproduce** — "What did you do right before the issue occurred?"
- **Expected vs actual behavior** — "I expected X to happen, but Y happened instead"
- **Screenshots or files** — Visual context speeds up diagnosis
- **Browser or device info** — "Using Chrome on Windows" or "iPhone 14 Pro"

<Callout variant="warning" title="Sensitive Data">
Do not include passwords, API keys, or credit card numbers in support tickets. If we need sensitive information, we'll provide a secure method to share it.
</Callout>

## Response Times

| Priority | Response Time | Resolution Time |
|----------|---------------|-----------------|
| Urgent   | 2 hours       | 24 hours        |
| High     | 8 hours       | 48 hours        |
| Normal   | 24 hours      | 3-5 days        |
| Low      | 48 hours      | 7 days          |

Priority is automatically assigned based on the issue type. If you believe your issue is urgent but was assigned Normal priority, reply to the confirmation email and we'll escalate.
