# Lane Analytics

<Callout variant="info">
Lane Analytics shows which origin-destination lanes are most profitable by comparing revenue, fuel costs, tolls, driver pay, and deadhead miles per lane.
</Callout>

## What this is

A profitability tool for analyzing freight lanes. See average revenue, expenses, and profit margin for every lane you've run in the past 90 days. Use this data to identify high-margin lanes worth pursuing and low-margin lanes to avoid.

<PlanBadge tier="business" /> This feature requires a Business plan or higher.

## How to use it

<StepFlow>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Access lane analytics">
    Go to **Lane Analytics** from the main menu. The default view shows top lanes by profit margin.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Review lane list">
    Each row shows one lane (e.g., "Chicago, IL → Dallas, TX") with load count, average revenue, average expenses, and profit margin percentage.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Click a lane for details">
    The lane detail view shows a breakdown: linehaul revenue, fuel costs, tolls, driver pay, maintenance, and deadhead miles.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Analyze trends">
    The trend chart shows how profit margin has changed over time for that lane. Look for lanes with declining margins—rates may not be keeping up with fuel costs.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Export data">
    Click **Export CSV** to download lane data for further analysis or to share with your freight broker partners.
  </StepFlow.Step>
</StepFlow>

## Good to know

<Callout variant="tip" title="Pro Tip">
Focus on lanes with 15%+ profit margin and high frequency (10+ loads per month). These are your bread-and-butter lanes—build dedicated relationships with shippers on these routes.
</Callout>

Lane analytics updates daily. Expenses are pulled from invoices, fuel logs, and payroll records. Ensure you're logging all costs to get accurate margin calculations.
