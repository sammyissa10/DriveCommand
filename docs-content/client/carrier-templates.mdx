# Dispatch Templates

<Callout variant="info">
Dispatch Templates let you save common lanes, routes, and dispatch configurations as reusable templates, reducing repetitive data entry.
</Callout>

## What this is

Pre-configured dispatch setups for routes you run frequently. If you regularly haul from Chicago to Dallas, or run a weekly dedicated lane, save those details once as a template. When you need to dispatch that route again, load the template and fill in only the unique details like pickup date or specific driver.

Templates store pickup/delivery addresses, typical rates, commodity types, and stop sequences. Perfect for dedicated lanes and recurring freight.

## How to use it

<StepFlow>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Create from an existing dispatch">
    When viewing a completed route or load, click **Save as Template**. Give it a descriptive name like "CHI-DAL Express" or "Weekly Walmart Run".
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Or build a template from scratch">
    Go to **Carrier → Templates** and click **New Template**. Configure the route stops, typical rate, and any standard notes or instructions.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Use a template for new loads">
    When creating a new load or route, click **Use Template** and select from your saved templates. DriveCommand auto-fills all template fields.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Customize as needed">
    Edit any pre-filled values (like pickup date or driver assignment) before saving the new dispatch. The template itself remains unchanged.
  </StepFlow.Step>
</StepFlow>

## Good to know

<Callout variant="tip" title="Pro Tip">
Create templates for your top 5 lanes. Even if the pickup and delivery addresses vary slightly, having the general route, rate structure, and commodity details pre-loaded saves significant time during busy dispatch hours.
</Callout>

Templates are tenant-wide, meaning all dispatchers on your team can access them. Use clear naming conventions so everyone knows which template to use.
