# Driver My Route

<Callout variant="info">
My Route shows your complete multi-stop assignment. View all loads in sequence, see truck details, and communicate with dispatch about route-specific issues — all in one place.
</Callout>

## Overview

When dispatch assigns you to a route with multiple stops, the My Route screen becomes your trip overview. It shows:

- **Route name and date** — e.g., "Chicago to Detroit Run - May 9"
- **Truck assignment** — Vehicle unit number, license plate, VIN
- **Loads timeline** — All pickups and deliveries in stop order
- **Route messages** — Thread for route-level communication

<PlanBadge tier="pro" /> This feature requires a Pro plan or higher.

## Route vs. Load

**What's the difference?**

- **Load** — A single shipment from one pickup to one delivery
- **Route** — A planned sequence of multiple stops (which may include multiple loads)

Example route: Pick up Load A in Chicago → Deliver Load A in Indianapolis → Pick up Load B in Indianapolis → Deliver Load B in Detroit.

You can access individual load details from the loads timeline, or view the entire route in one screen.

## Loads timeline

The timeline shows all stops in order:

Each stop card shows:

- **Stop number and type** — "Stop 1: Pickup" or "Stop 2: Delivery"
- **Address** — City and state
- **Scheduled time** — When you need to arrive
- **Load ID** — Reference for this shipment
- **Contact** — Name and phone (tap to call)
- **BOL/PO numbers** — Document references

### Stop status indicators

- **Green checkmark** — Completed stop
- **Blue dot** — Current stop (where you should be now)
- **Gray dot** — Upcoming stop

## Truck details card

The truck card shows:

- **Unit number** — Fleet identifier (e.g., "Truck 42")
- **Make/model** — e.g., "2022 Freightliner Cascadia"
- **License plate** — For reference during inspections
- **Odometer reading** — Last reported mileage
- **Fuel level** — Last reported percentage

Truck details are read-only. If you notice incorrect information (e.g., wrong unit number), contact dispatch via route messages.

## Route messages

At the bottom of the My Route screen, you'll see a message thread specific to this route. Use it for:

- **ETA updates** — "Traffic on I-94, will be 30 min late to Stop 3"
- **Route questions** — "Stop 2 contact not answering, should I wait?"
- **Issue reports** — "Shipper says Load B isn't ready, waiting on paperwork"

<Callout variant="tip" title="Route vs. Load messages">
**Route messages** are for multi-stop coordination and trip-level issues. **Load messages** (accessible from individual load screens) are for single-shipment questions. If in doubt, use route messages — dispatch will redirect you if needed.
</Callout>

## Access My Route

There are two ways to open My Route:

1. **From Dashboard** — If you're assigned to a route, a "My Route" button appears on the dashboard
2. **From Loads tab** — Tap the active load card, then tap "View Full Route" at the top

If you're assigned to a single load (not part of a multi-stop route), the My Route screen won't appear.

## Sequence order

Routes are pre-planned by dispatch. You cannot reorder stops or skip ahead. The timeline always shows stops in the sequence dispatch created.

If you need to change the stop order due to road closures, shipper delays, or other issues, contact dispatch immediately via route messages. Only dispatchers can modify route sequences.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Do not skip stops">
Skipping stops or delivering out of sequence can cause billing errors, missed appointments, and customer complaints. Always follow the planned sequence unless dispatch explicitly approves a change.
</Callout>

## Navigation

From the My Route screen, tap **Start Navigation** to open your preferred navigation app (Google Maps, Waze, or Apple Maps) with the next stop pre-loaded.

You can change your navigation preference in **More → Settings → Navigation**.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why don't I see a My Route screen?**

My Route only appears when you're assigned to a multi-stop route. Single-load assignments don't have a separate route screen — just use the load detail screen.

**Can I see routes I've already completed?**

Not yet. Route history is planned for a future release. Currently, you can view completed loads in **Loads → History** tab.

**What if one stop gets delayed?**

Contact dispatch immediately via route messages. They can notify customers, adjust ETAs, and potentially reorder stops if needed. Do not make stop order changes yourself.
