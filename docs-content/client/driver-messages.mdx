# Driver Messaging

<Callout variant="info">
Driver Messaging keeps you connected with dispatch. Send and receive messages about your current load, get real-time updates, and ask questions without leaving the app.
</Callout>

## Overview

The Messages screen shows all your message threads with dispatch. Messages are organized by load, so you can easily find communication about a specific shipment.

<PlanBadge tier="pro" /> This feature requires a Pro plan or higher.

## Message types

### Load-scoped messages

When you're working on a load, all messages about that load appear in a dedicated thread. Tap the load to see:

- Delivery instructions from dispatch
- Customer contact updates
- Rate changes or accessorial charges
- Special handling requirements

Load messages are archived after delivery but remain accessible in your message history.

### Broadcast messages

Fleet-wide announcements from dispatch appear with a megaphone icon:

- Policy updates
- Weather alerts
- Holiday schedules
- General company news

You cannot reply to broadcast messages.

## Send a message

<StepFlow>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Open Messages">
    Tap **Messages** in the bottom navigation.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Select a thread">
    Tap the load or route you want to message about. Or tap **New Message** to start a thread.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Type your message">
    Tap the text input at the bottom. Type your message (up to 500 characters).
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Send">
    Tap the send button (paper plane icon). Your message delivers instantly if you're online, or queues for sending when you reconnect.
  </StepFlow.Step>
</StepFlow>

<Callout variant="tip" title="Pro Tip">
Be specific in your messages. Instead of "Running late," say "I-90 traffic accident, ETA now 3:45 PM instead of 3:00 PM." Specific details help dispatch notify customers and avoid upset.
</Callout>

## Unread badges

Unread message counts appear as red badges:

- **Messages tab icon** — Total unread across all threads
- **Thread list** — Unread count per load/route

Messages are marked as read automatically when you open a thread. The badge updates within 30 seconds.

### Push notifications

When you receive a new message while the app is closed or in the background, you'll get a push notification:

- **Banner** — Shows sender (Dispatch) and message preview
- **Sound/vibration** — Standard notification alert
- **Badge** — App icon badge count

Tap the notification to open the thread directly.

<Callout variant="info">
Enable push notifications in your phone's Settings → DriveCommand → Notifications. Without notification permission, you'll only see messages when you open the app.
</Callout>

## Message polling

The app checks for new messages every 30 seconds while the Messages screen is open. When you navigate away, polling stops to save battery. New messages still trigger push notifications.

If you need instant delivery, enable push notifications. Polling is a backup for when notifications are delayed.

## Offline messaging

If you lose cellular signal:

- **Sent messages queue locally** and deliver when you reconnect
- **New messages won't arrive** until you're back online
- **Blue "Syncing..." banner** appears when queued messages are flushing

Queued messages send automatically within 60 seconds of reconnection.

## Message etiquette

- **Reply promptly** — Dispatch needs timely updates to manage customer expectations
- **Be professional** — Messages are logged and may be reviewed by management
- **Use the right thread** — Load messages for load questions, route messages for route issues
- **Don't spam** — If dispatch doesn't reply immediately, they're likely helping another driver

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I message other drivers?**

Not yet. Driver-to-driver messaging is planned for a future release. Currently, all messages go through dispatch.

**How long are messages stored?**

Messages are stored indefinitely. You can scroll back through old threads to review past communication about delivered loads.

**What if I don't get a reply?**

Dispatchers juggle many drivers. If your message is urgent (breakdown, accident, customer issue), call dispatch instead of waiting for a message reply. Phone numbers are in **More → Settings → Support**.

**Can dispatch see when I read a message?**

Yes. Read receipts are enabled. When you open a thread, dispatch sees a "Read" indicator. This helps them know whether you've seen important updates.
