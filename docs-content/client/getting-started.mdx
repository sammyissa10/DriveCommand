# Getting Started with DriveCommand

## What is DriveCommand?

DriveCommand is a fleet management platform built for small-to-mid-size trucking companies. It brings together everything you need to run your operation: dispatch, driver management, compliance tracking, invoicing, HOS logging, GPS tracking, and communication — all in one system.

**Built for owner-operators and small fleets** who don't have time for complex enterprise software but need more than spreadsheets and phone calls.

## What problems does it solve?

| Problem | How DriveCommand Helps |
|---------|------------------------|
| **Dispatch chaos** — loads assigned via text, phone, sticky notes | All loads in one place with status tracking, driver assignment, and push notifications |
| **Compliance headaches** — expired documents discovered during an audit | Document expiration dashboard with automated alerts before things expire |
| **Missing paperwork** — PODs lost between driver and office | Drivers upload photos and documents directly to the load record in the app |
| **HOS violations** — drivers running out of hours on the road | Real-time HOS tracking with countdown timers and violation warnings |
| **Cash flow problems** — invoices sent late or not at all | One-click invoice generation from delivered loads with aging reports |
| **No visibility** — calling drivers to ask "where are you?" | Live GPS map showing all trucks in real-time |

## Who uses DriveCommand?

**Owners and Dispatchers** use the web dashboard to:
- Create and manage loads
- Track fleet location and status
- Monitor compliance and document expirations
- Generate invoices and track payments
- View analytics and reports

**Drivers** use the mobile app to:
- See assigned loads and routes
- Update load status (picked up, in transit, delivered)
- Log Hours of Service (HOS)
- Complete pre-trip inspections and checklists
- Upload documents and POD photos
- Message with dispatch

## Key concepts

Before setting up, understand these terms:

- **Load** — A single shipment from pickup to delivery. Loads have a customer, origin, destination, rate, and assigned driver/truck.
- **Route** — A sequence of stops. Routes can contain multiple loads (multi-stop).
- **Tenant** — Your company account. All your trucks, drivers, loads, and data are scoped to your tenant.
- **Checklist** — A sequence of steps (inspection items, document uploads, signatures) that drivers complete. Can auto-start on dispatch.

## Quick Setup Guide

Complete these steps in order. You'll be dispatching your first load in under 30 minutes.

### Step 1: Add Your Trucks

Before you can dispatch, DriveCommand needs to know about your equipment.

<StepFlow>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Go to Trucks">
    Click **Trucks** in the sidebar (under Drivers & Trucks).
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Click New Truck">
    The truck form opens.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Enter truck details">
    - **Unit number** — your internal identifier (e.g., "T-101")
    - **Make and model** — "Freightliner Cascadia", "Kenworth T680", etc.
    - **VIN** — for compliance tracking
    - **License plate** and **state**
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Save">
    Your truck appears in the fleet list with "Ready to Use" status.
  </StepFlow.Step>
</StepFlow>

<Callout variant="info">
You can add trucks later as your fleet grows. Start with the trucks you're using today.
</Callout>

<FeatureCard slug="trucks" />

### Step 2: Invite Your Drivers

Drivers use the DriveCommand mobile app to receive loads, update statuses, and log hours.

<StepFlow>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Go to Drivers">
    Click **Drivers** in the sidebar.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Click Invite Driver">
    Enter the driver's email address.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Set permissions">
    Choose what the driver can access (loads, documents, messages). Most carriers give drivers full access.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Send invitation">
    The driver receives an email with setup instructions and a link to download the app.
  </StepFlow.Step>
</StepFlow>

**What the driver does:**
1. Opens the invitation email
2. Downloads DriveCommand from App Store or Google Play
3. Signs in with the email you invited
4. Sees their dashboard and any assigned loads

<FeatureCard slug="drivers" />

### Step 3: Add Your First Customer

Customers are the shippers or brokers who give you loads.

<StepFlow>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Go to CRM">
    Click **CRM** in the sidebar (under Settings & Setup).
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Click New Customer">
    Enter customer details: company name, contact person, phone, email.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Save">
    The customer is now available when creating loads.
  </StepFlow.Step>
</StepFlow>

<Callout variant="tip" title="Pro Tip">
Add your most frequent customers first. You can add more later as you book new freight.
</Callout>

<FeatureCard slug="crm" />

### Step 4: Create Your First Load

Now you're ready to dispatch!

<StepFlow>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Go to Loads">
    Click **Loads** in the sidebar.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Click New Load">
    The load creation form opens.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Select customer">
    Choose the customer you just added.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Enter pickup">
    Address, contact name, phone, pickup date/time.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Enter delivery">
    Destination address, contact, delivery date/time.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Add rate">
    Enter what you're charging for this load (line haul rate).
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Assign driver and truck">
    Select which driver will haul it and which truck they'll use.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Save">
    The load is created. The driver receives a push notification and sees the load in their app immediately.
  </StepFlow.Step>
</StepFlow>

**What the driver sees:**
- Push notification: "New load assigned: Load #1234"
- Load card on their dashboard with pickup address and time
- Tapping the load shows full details: pickup, delivery, customer contact, BOL if attached

<FeatureCard slug="loads" />

### Step 5: Track Progress

Once the driver starts working the load:

1. **Driver updates status** — At Pickup → In Transit → At Delivery → Delivered
2. **You see updates in real-time** — Load list and dashboard show current status
3. **Live Map** — Click the map icon to see the truck's current location
4. **Driver captures POD** — Photos and signatures uploaded to the load record

<FeatureCard slug="live-map" />

### Step 6: Invoice the Customer

After the driver marks the load delivered:

<StepFlow>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Go to Invoices">
    Click **Invoices** in the sidebar.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Click New Invoice">
    Select the delivered load from the list.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Review details">
    Customer, rate, and load details auto-populate. Add line items if needed (detention, fuel surcharge, accessorials).
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Generate and send">
    Click **Create Invoice**, then email the PDF to your customer.
  </StepFlow.Step>
</StepFlow>
