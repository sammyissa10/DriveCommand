# Client Management

<Callout variant="info">
Client Management centralizes all your shipper and broker relationships, tracking contact details, load history, and revenue by customer.
</Callout>

## What this is

A dedicated customer relationship tool for your freight business. Store shipper and broker contacts, track load volume per customer, monitor revenue trends, and keep notes on customer preferences. This is where you manage the business relationships that keep your trucks moving.

Different from the legacy CRM, Carrier Client Management is optimized specifically for trucking operations with fields like MC numbers, preferred lanes, and payment terms.

## How to use it

<StepFlow>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Add a new client">
    Go to **Carrier → Clients** and click **New Client**. Enter the company name, MC number, contact person, phone, and email.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Record client details">
    Fill in optional fields like billing address, payment terms (Net 30/60/90), preferred lanes, and any special handling notes.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="View client profile">
    Click any client name to see their full profile: total loads hauled, total revenue, recent load history, and outstanding invoices.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Track load history">
    The client detail page shows all loads for that customer with status, dates, revenue, and links to invoices. Use this to analyze customer profitability.
  </StepFlow.Step>
</StepFlow>

## Good to know

<Callout variant="tip" title="Pro Tip">
Use the revenue tracking to identify your most valuable customers. Focus on building stronger relationships with high-volume shippers by offering priority dispatch or volume discounts.
</Callout>

Client records automatically link to loads when you select the customer during load creation. Revenue totals update in real-time as invoices are created.
