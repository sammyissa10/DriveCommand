# Incident Reporting

<Callout variant="info">
The Incident Reporting tool lets you quickly document accidents, vehicle damage, safety hazards, or other events while you're on the road. Photos, location data, and timestamps are captured automatically.
</Callout>

## Overview

Report incidents directly from the mobile app. All incident reports are reviewed by your fleet manager and logged for insurance, safety compliance, and DOT audits.

Use incident reporting for:

- **Accidents** — Collisions with other vehicles, property damage
- **Vehicle issues** — Breakdowns, tire blowouts, mechanical failures
- **Safety hazards** — Unsafe road conditions, cargo shifts, equipment malfunctions
- **Cargo damage** — Damaged freight discovered during loading or delivery
- **Citations** — Traffic tickets, roadside inspections, violations

<PlanBadge tier="pro" /> This feature requires a Pro plan or higher.

## Report an incident

<StepFlow>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Open the incident form">
    Navigate to **More → Incidents → New Incident** (or tap the incident shortcut on the dashboard if one is available).
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Select severity">
    Use the traffic light severity toggle:
    - **Minor** (Green) — No injuries, minimal damage, no tow required
    - **Moderate** (Yellow) — Vehicle damage requiring repair, possible tow
    - **Severe** (Red) — Injuries, major damage, emergency services called
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Choose category">
    Select the incident type from the dropdown:
    - Accident
    - Breakdown
    - Cargo Damage
    - Safety Hazard
    - Traffic Citation
    - Other
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Describe what happened">
    Write a detailed description (minimum 20 characters). Include:
    - What happened and when
    - Location details (mile marker, intersection, etc.)
    - Other parties involved (if applicable)
    - Weather/road conditions
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Capture photos">
    Tap **Add Photo** to take pictures with your camera:
    - Vehicle damage (all angles)
    - Other vehicles involved
    - Road conditions or hazards
    - Cargo damage

    You can upload up to 5 photos per incident.
  </StepFlow.Step>
  <StepFlow.Step title="Submit">
    Tap **Submit Incident**. Your report is sent immediately to dispatch and fleet management. You'll receive a confirmation with the incident ID.
  </StepFlow.Step>
</StepFlow>

<Callout variant="warning" title="Report immediately">
Always report incidents as soon as it's safe to do so. Delayed reporting can complicate insurance claims and DOT investigations. If you're injured or in an unsafe location, call 911 first, then submit the incident report when you're secure.
</Callout>

## Severity levels

Choose the severity level that best matches the situation:

### Minor (Green)

- No injuries
- Minimal vehicle damage (scratches, dents)
- No tow truck needed
- No police report filed
- Examples: Backing into a pole, minor fender bender, tire damage from road debris

### Moderate (Yellow)

- No serious injuries (minor cuts/bruises okay)
- Vehicle damage requiring repair
- May need tow truck
- Police report may be filed
- Examples: Side-swipe collision, blown engine, cargo shift causing trailer damage

### Severe (Red)

- Any injury requiring medical attention
- Major vehicle damage (totaled, fire, rollover)
- Tow truck required
- Police/emergency services on scene
- Examples: Multi-vehicle accident, rollover, hit pedestrian, hazmat spill

<Callout variant="danger" title="Severe incidents">
For severe incidents: Call 911, then your fleet manager immediately. Submit the incident report after emergency services arrive. Do not move the vehicle unless instructed by police or it's blocking traffic and unsafe to remain.
</Callout>

## Photo capture best practices

Good incident photos help with insurance claims and investigations. Capture:

1. **Wide shot** — Shows full scene, vehicle position, road context
2. **Vehicle damage** — Close-ups of all damage from multiple angles
3. **Other vehicles** — License plates, damage, position
4. **Road hazards** — Potholes, debris, signage, weather conditions
5. **Documentation** — Police report, citation, inspection form (if applicable)

Photos are automatically tagged with GPS coordinates and timestamp.

## After submitting

Once submitted:

- **Fleet manager is notified immediately** via push notification and email
- **Incident ID is generated** (e.g., INC-2024-001234)
- **You receive confirmation** in-app and via email
- **Follow-up may be required** — Manager may request additional photos or details

You can view all your submitted incidents in **More → Incidents → History**.

## Manager review

Your fleet manager reviews every incident report and may:

- Contact you for clarification
- File an insurance claim
- Schedule vehicle for repair
- Update DOT safety records
- Coordinate with authorities or other parties

You'll receive updates via push notification as the incident is processed.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I edit an incident after submitting?**

No. Incident reports are immutable once submitted to maintain audit integrity. If you need to add information, contact your fleet manager — they can append notes to the incident record.

**What if I forgot to report an incident immediately?**

Submit the report as soon as you remember. In the description, note the actual incident date/time and explain the delay (e.g., "Occurred May 5 at 2pm, reported late due to hospital visit").

**Do I need to report minor incidents?**

Yes. Even minor incidents should be reported. Your fleet manager decides whether an incident requires insurance filing or DOT logging. Unreported incidents discovered later can cause insurance claim denials.

**What happens to my CSA score?**

Incident reports themselves don't affect your CSA score. However, if an incident results in a citation or crash report filed with FMCSA, that will appear on your CSA record. DriveCommand does not control CSA scoring.
